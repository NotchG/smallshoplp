## Personality
A big, polished two-level cafe-eatery that works as a proper place to eat, a work-from-cafe spot and a hangout, open from breakfast coffee to late evening (hours, reviews 1, 2, 4). Downstairs is a grey-concrete coffee bar with a lit pastry case; upstairs is a tall A-frame room with timber rafters, a glass wall onto trees and lots of plants (photos).
- The staff are known for going a step further: adjusting a dish on request, offering to take guests' photos, clearing tables quickly (review 1)
- They screen football matches for group viewing ("nobar") during the World Cup (review 3)
- [MOCKUP] OSMO sees itself as "the nice cafe in East Jakarta you don't have to drive into town for" (based on review 2 saying such a cafe is rare in the area)
