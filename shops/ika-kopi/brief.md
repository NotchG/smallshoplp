## Personality
A spacious, bright, properly cold coffee shop on Jl. Raya Ciracas that takes its coffee seriously and is just as
good for a long work session: wide tables, a plug at every table, cushioned chairs and planter boxes full of
greenery (reviews 1, photos).
- Manual brew comes in a glass carafe on a wooden tray branded with the logo, with a printed card describing the bean (photos)
- Open 07:00–23:00 every day, so it covers the first coffee of the day through to late work sessions (hours)
- Clean and well kept: reviewers single out the toilet (reviews 1)
- The brand promise is in the tagline: "Keharuman & Cita Rasa", aroma and taste (photos)
- [MOCKUP] A calm third place in Ciracas: come to work, stay for a good cup.

Calm, polished and quietly proud of the coffee. A step more refined than a warung kopi, never snobbish. It leads
with aroma, taste and comfort, and explains brew methods in plain words.

- Example: "Butuh tempat kerja yang dingin dan tenang? Setiap meja punya colokan."
