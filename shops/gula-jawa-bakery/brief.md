# Gula Jawa Bakery

The listing name is all caps: "GULA JAWA BAKERY". Use "Gula Jawa Bakery" on the page (listing). The neon sign in the shop writes "Gula Jawa" in a looping script (photos).

## Personality
A busy Klender bakery near the railway with an open kitchen, so you can watch the bread and buns being baked (review 3). It went viral for its abon roll, and the good stuff sells out early (reviews 2, 3).
Inside: deep maroon walls, wood-grain panelling, a white neon "Gula Jawa" sign over slate-blue bench seating, teak chairs, and copper cooling racks in the display case (photos). The dine-in area is small (review 2).
- [MOCKUP] Proud of its name: gula jawa (palm sugar) as a quiet thread through the menu and the page.
- [MOCKUP] Unfussy and a bit cheeky about selling out: "datang pagi" is part of the charm, not an apology.

## Audience
- People chasing the famous abon roll, willing to come early for it (reviews 2, 3)
- Locals in Klender looking for an everyday bakery (review 4)
- Mostly takeaway buyers, with a few tables for eating in (reviews 1, 2)
- [MOCKUP] Commuters on the railway side of Klender picking up breakfast bread on the way to work (the shop opens at 07:00)

## Signature products
- Abon roll, also known as abon gulung (floss roll): the bestseller, and it sells out fast (reviews 2, 3)
- Laminated pastry: buttery, crisp outside and soft inside, and big for the price (review 2)
- Twisted, glossy laminated pastries with a dark filling (photos). The menu name isn't known.
- Round pastry-donuts with colourful glazes and toppings: pink, blue, chocolate, cocoa-dusted, cookie crumble (photos)
- Tiramisu donut, chocolate sourdough, cheese bread, srikaya bread (review 4)
- Roti chicken karage and a cheesecake brownie (review 1)
- Cheesecake by the slice. One reviewer paid about Rp 50rb for a thick slice (review 1)
- Split buns heaped with a creamy, shredded filling (photos). The menu name isn't known.
- [MOCKUP] A few palm-sugar bakes to match the name, e.g. roti gula jawa, bolu gula jawa, and kopi susu gula aren
- [MOCKUP] Everyday Indonesian breads alongside the pastry, e.g. roti sobek, roti cokelat keju, roti pisang cokelat
- [MOCKUP] Example prices: roti manis from Rp 12rb, abon gulung Rp 22rb, pastry and pastry-donuts from Rp 25rb, cheesecake slice Rp 50rb

## What customers praise
- The abon roll lives up to its reputation (reviews 2, 3)
- Buttery, crisp, generous pastry that's good value for its size (review 2)
- An open kitchen where you can see everything baked fresh (review 3)
- Staff offered to warm up the bread and cake before serving, and service has improved (review 1)
- A solid neighbourhood bakery for Klender (review 4)
- Note, and the reason the rating is 4.3: several reviewers found trays empty, by mid-morning on a busy day and again late in the afternoon (reviews 2, 4, 5). Some found the taste only average or the toppings and fillings thin (reviews 1, 4). Items were listed at the cashier that weren't available, and one cheesecake was still frozen (reviews 1, 5). Seating is limited, tables weren't cleared, and the shop was thinly staffed at busy times (reviews 2, 5).
  - For the planner: don't promise a full display all day or overpraise the cheesecake. Lead with the abon roll, the pastry and the open kitchen, and be upfront that it's best to come early. The display case even carries a "Dummy" sign on some pieces (photos).
