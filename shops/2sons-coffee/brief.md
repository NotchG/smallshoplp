## Personality
A calm, gallery-clean coffee shop on JGC Boulevard that takes its coffee seriously without being
stiff: reviewers call the coffee properly made, the staff friendly and helpful, the mood chill
(reviews 2, 3, 4). Inside it's cool grey and white, with glowing shelves of books, vinyl records,
a turntable and designer bear figures, fresh flowers, and an oak communal table (photos).
"Specialty coffee" is lettered on the glass door (photos).
- [MOCKUP] A place that's as easy at 7am for a quick takeaway as it is for a long afternoon with a laptop or a game of mahjong.
- [MOCKUP] Quietly design-minded: the shelves, records and art are part of why people stay.

## Audience
- People who work from cafes (WFC) (review 1)

- Coffee drinkers in the JGC area who want coffee made properly (review 3)
