# PRD: De 92 Degrees mockup site

Shop slug `92-degrees-coffee-resto`. Listed on Google as "92 Degrees Coffee & Resto"; the shop's own
menu, poster and Instagram use the "de 92°" logo, so the page is named **De 92 Degrees** (brief, Name note).
Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout, `lang="id"` (brief, Tone of voice).

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes De 92 Degrees' owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

## The shop in one paragraph
De 92 Degrees is a four-floor cafe-restaurant at Jl. Delima Raya No.6, Malaka Sari, Duren Sawit,
open every day 09:00–22:00 (Friday and Saturday until 23:00), with the tagline "Coffee, Food & Music".
Each floor has its own job: floor 2 is non-smoking, floor 3 has indoor smoking plus an event space for
up to 100 people, floor 4 is a smoking area with the best sunset view, and a lift runs to floors 3 and 4.
The menu mixes Indonesian rice plates (Bebek Bangsawan, Dori Matah) with fresh pasta and a Klepon Cake
dessert. The space is polished: honey oak, sage banquettes in arched niches, walnut chairs, terrazzo.
Friends, families, people working from the cafe and community groups come here, and reviewers praise the
roomy, clean space, the friendly staff, the signature coffee and the big portions. Google rating 4.8 from
920 reviews.
