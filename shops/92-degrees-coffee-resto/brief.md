## Personality
A big, bright, carefully designed hangout over four floors, where people come for coffee in the morning, long lunches, work sessions and evening gatherings with live music (reviews 1, 2, 4). The look is polished: warm wood, sage-green banquettes, arches and terrazzo (photos). The menu mixes Indonesian plates with Western pastas, and its own tagline calls the klepon cake a "modern yet traditional" take (photos).
- Each floor does something different: floor 2 is non-smoking, floor 3 has indoor smoking plus the event space, floor 4 is a smoking area with the best sunset view (reviews 2)
- A lift goes up to floors 3 and 4 (reviews 2, 3)
- Live music comes with the VIP room booking (reviews 1), and the logo tagline is "Coffee, Food & Music" (photos)
- [MOCKUP] Live music on Friday and Saturday nights, when the shop stays open until 23:00
- [MOCKUP] "From breakfast coffee to a sunset on the rooftop" is the shop's whole-day story
