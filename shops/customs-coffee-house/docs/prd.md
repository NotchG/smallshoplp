# PRD: Customs Coffee House mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Customs Coffee House's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (the mustard
fascia, the circled-S wordmark, the bean jars, the checkerboard tables), and the checklist
questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours and the draft banner.

## The shop in one paragraph
A garage-flavoured neighbourhood coffee house at Jl. Bujana Tirta 2 No.4A, Pisangan Timur,
Pulo Gadung (listing). Mustard-yellow fascia, teal lettering and awning, green railings,
checkerboard tables, club stickers on the counter, and a row of single-origin bean jars behind
the bar labelled Lombok, Brasil, Guatemala, Gayo, Wamena, Bali and Wine (photos). People praise
strong, good coffee at a fair price, the wide single-origin choice, genuine friendly service,
free WiFi, good music, and a relaxed place for friends, studying, quick meetings and work;
cyclists (goweser) are welcome (reviews 1–5). Open every day until 01:00: Mon–Sat 08:00–01:00,
Sun 09:00–01:00 (hours). Rated 4.6 from 334 Google reviews (listing). House rule: no outside
food or drinks (reviews 3; photos).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out: a live "open now" status, the
name's story line (until the owner confirms it), owner-4.jpg (guests' faces) and every
customer photo (`photo-*.jpg`).

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is "Customs Coffee House" the name you want on the site (rather than "Customs Coffee")?
2. Does the name "Customs" come from custom cars and motorbikes? From the customs-service patches on the red wall? (We'll add one line on the site once you tell us.)
3. Is the hero line "Kopi single origin, buka sampai jam 1 malam" right for you?
4. Does 0812-9154-8783 take WhatsApp?
5. Are the seven taste notes on the jars right? (Correct any.)
6. Is the "Wine" jar a wine-process coffee?
7. Do you brew any bean from the shelf manually, V60 or tubruk?
8. Is the green drink an Es Green Tea Latte, and do you call the drip "Vietnam Drip" on your menu?
9. Do you serve Kopi Susu and Kopi Susu Gula Aren?
10. Is this food list right: Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng, Indomie Telur, Roti Bakar, Pisang Goreng, Kentang Goreng?
11. Are these example prices right: Vietnam Drip Rp 22rb, manual brew Rp 25rb, Es Green Tea Latte Rp 25rb, Nasi Goreng Rp 28rb?
12. Is Customs a regular meet-up spot for car, scooter and cycling clubs, and can clubs arrange a kopdar on the terrace through WhatsApp?
13. Is the casual "kamu" voice right, or would you prefer "Anda"?
14. May we use your photo of guests at the long terrace table (their faces show)? It is left off for now.
