## Personality
A small retro coffee spot whose name means roughly "old face": the logo is a vintage-style portrait of a
woman in a cream oval, set on a terracotta breeze-block wall with an arched blue doorway (photos). Inside
it's cobalt blue with a blue-and-white checkerboard counter, a hand-lettered chalkboard menu, an old
pendulum clock, rows of retro poster cards, and a shelf with a record player, old vinyl, cassettes and
film cameras (photos). Out back there's a black-and-white checkerboard patio with stools on gravel and a
staircase up to a covered upper deck (photos). Reviewers call it comfy, with a great choice of music
(reviews 1, 3).
- [MOCKUP] Nostalgic without being dusty: old songs, old spelling, young crowd.
- [MOCKUP] The bottled coffee's old-spelling name ("Toean Njonja") sets the playful tone for the brand.
