# PRD: Paras Lawas Kopi mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Paras Lawas Kopi's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All page copy is Bahasa Indonesia (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours, alt text and the draft banner.

## The shop in one paragraph
Paras Lawas Kopi is a small retro coffee shop on Jl. Swakarsa IV No. 2 in Pondok Kelapa, Duren Sawit,
Jakarta Timur. It is open every day 10.30–22.30 and rated 5.0 from 103 Google reviews (listing). The name
means roughly "old face". The sign is a vintage portrait of a woman on a cream oval, mounted on a terracotta
breeze-block wall beside an arched blue doorway. Inside are cobalt walls, a blue-and-white checkerboard
counter, a hand-lettered chalkboard menu, an old pendulum clock, retro poster cards, and a shelf with a
record player, old vinyl, cassettes and film cameras. Out back is a black-and-white checkerboard patio and
stairs up to a covered deck (photos). The board sells coffee (24–27rb), snacks (Sosis Solo, Dimsum, Kentang
Goreng, Singkong Keju), meals (Mi Korean Edition, Mi Nusantara, Mentaiki) and cheap bundles from 22rb.
The shop has its own bottled coffee with the Paras Lawas label, Kopi Toean Njonja (photos). Reviewers call it comfy for me-time or
hanging out with friends, like the music, and say the non-coffee drinks are good (reviews 1, 3).

## Visitors and what they need

| Local looking for a place to hang out tonight | know if it's open now and until when | see "Buka setiap hari 10.30–22.30" and the live status (clock) in the first screen |
| Group of friends on a budget | see what a visit costs | read the board prices and the three bundles (from 22rb) without hunting |
| Non-coffee drinker | know there's something for them | find the non-coffee line and es teh on the menu board |
| First-timer coming from Google Maps | find the door | tap "Petunjuk Arah" in the hero and again by the map and address |
| Someone wanting coffee at home | learn about the bottled coffee | see Kopi Toean Njonja, and ask about it via WhatsApp |
| Regular | check a price or contact the shop | call, WhatsApp, or open Instagram from the close of the page |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real product photos (illustrations only,
until the owner sends their own), multiple pages, a live music or playlist player, member sign-up.

## Open questions for the owner
Every [MOCKUP] item from brief.md and this PRD, as a yes/no question. These become `owner-checklist.md`.
1. Are the board prices on the page still current (Sosis Solo 18rb, Dimsum 22rb, Kentang Goreng 17rb, Singkong Keju 18rb, Extra Saus 3rb, Mi 19rb, Mentaiki Ayam 20rb, Mentaiki Dori 22rb, Extra Mi 6rb, coffee 24–27rb, bundles 22/23/24/26rb)?
2. Are your coffees Es Kopi Susu Paras Lawas, Kopi Susu Gula Aren, Americano and Cappuccino? (If not, which names go with 24–27rb?)
3. Are the two 9rb drinks Es Teh Manis and Air Mineral?
4. Do you serve matcha latte, coklat and red velvet as non-coffee drinks?
5. Is the 24rb Mentaiki bundle the Ayam version and the 26rb one the Dori version?
6. Is Kopi Toean Njonja sold over the counter to take home?
7. Does it come in 1-litre bottles? What price should the page show (or keep "tanya di kasir")?
8. Does 0851-3428-6223 take WhatsApp messages?
9. For the membership offer, is "tanya di kasir" right, or do you want the actual deal on the page?
10. Is the Instagram handle @paraslawaskopi correct?
11. Do you like the tagline "Ngopi santai, lagu lawas, ngobrol sampai malam."?
12. Is the casual "kamu" tone right, with old spelling in only two places ("Toean Njonja", "Mampir Doeloe")?
13. Does "nostalgic without being dusty: old songs, old spelling, young crowd" describe Paras Lawas?
14. Does the old spelling of "Toean Njonja" set the playful tone you want for the brand?
15. Are students and young workers from Pondok Kelapa and Duren Sawit your main crowd?
16. Is the "Sisi A" tracklist idea for the shop's corners (records, music, patio, upstairs deck) OK?
17. The food and bottle are drawn as illustrations. Would you send your own product photos to replace them?
18. Is it OK to use your counter photo cropped to the menu board, lamps and clock (no customers or staff visible)?
