# PRD: D'rooftop & Garage Brew House mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes the owner of D'rooftop & Garage Brew House say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their terracotta
wall, their sign plates, their rooftop, their layered kopi susu, their own mural slogan), and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (copy, buttons, hours, draft banner, `lang="id"`).

## The shop in one paragraph
D'rooftop & Garage Brew House is a neighbourhood coffee shop at Jl. K No. 18, Cipinang Muara,
Jatinegara (listing), open every day 15.00–23.30 (hours). It has two floors: a ground-floor
"garage" with round rattan chairs, navy polka-dot cushions, street-art murals and a hand-painted
sign, and an open rooftop with artificial turf, low wooden lesehan tables on mats, string lights
and potted trees (photos). Its own rooftop mural says "Kopi adalah inspirasi hati" (photos), and
the rooftop takes group bookings (photos). Locals come for a cosy, affordable place to hang out;
reviewers call it a hidden gem where they feel at home, with friendly staff and good coffee
(reviews 1–5). Google rating 4.8 from 226 reviews (listing). The way in is a narrow alley lined
with parked motorbikes (photos), so finding it is part of the visit.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends or forms (booking is a WhatsApp link only), CMS, accounts,
analytics, a live open/closed indicator, real photos beyond owner-1.jpg and owner-2.jpg
(illustrations fill the other slots), customer photos (photo-1/2/3 are reference only), an
Instagram link (handle unreadable on the chalkboard), listing the musholla, car-parking
information, multiple pages.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Can customers message you on WhatsApp at 0813-1431-7421 for table and group bookings?
2. Is it fine for the site to talk to visitors as "kamu" rather than "Anda"?
3. Is the layered glass in your photo (black coffee over sweet milk) OK to call "Kopi Susu Garage"? If it has a real name, what is it?
4. Can you send your real menu and prices so we can replace the examples? Until then, are these close: Kopi Susu Garage Rp 15rb, Es Kopi Susu Gula Aren Rp 18rb, Kopi Tubruk Lampung Rp 10rb, V60 Rp 22rb, Teh Tarik / Coklat / Matcha Latte Rp 15rb–20rb, Pisang Goreng / Kentang Goreng / Roti Bakar / Indomie Telor Rp 12rb–20rb?
5. Are the beans in the "LAM…" jar from Lampung?
6. Do you serve V60 and kopi tubruk on request?
7. Do the example prices match your price level (the listing says moderate; a reviewer calls it very affordable)?
8. Is it right to describe the Garage as good for an afternoon coffee and the Rooftop as good for groups sitting lesehan and talking for hours?
9. Is "come for coffee in the afternoon, stay on the rooftop into the night" a fair way to describe the shop?
10. Do you take rooftop bookings for buka bersama, birthdays and community meet-ups?
11. Should the page say "Jam buka bisa berubah selama Ramadan"? Are Ramadan hours 17.00–00.00 as on your chalkboard?

14. Is there a musholla on the rooftop that we can list?
15. What is your Instagram handle, so we can add the link?
16. Can you send more photos (the Garage, the rooftop at night, the drinks and snacks) to replace the illustrations?
