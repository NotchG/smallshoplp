A calm, clean cafe in a JGC shophouse with a menu people find interesting, food they say is all
good, and a private room for meetings (reviews 1, 4, 5). Warm wood slats, arched niches and
arched glass doors, linear pendant lights, open black-steel shelves of syrup bottles, an orchid
on the bar, and a backlit line-art logo of an arch with leaves (photos).
