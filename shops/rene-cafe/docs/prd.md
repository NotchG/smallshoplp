# PRD: Rene Cafe mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Rene Cafe's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (copy, buttons, hours, draft banner, `lang="id"`).
Dish names stay as reviewers write them until the owner's menu confirms them.

## The shop in one paragraph
Rene Cafe is a cafe in Pondok Ranggon, Cipayung, Jakarta Timur, open every day 10:00–22:00 at a
moderate price level, rated 4.2 from 618 Google reviews (listing, hours). It is a brick-and-glass
building (exposed red brick, a steep gabled roof on black steel trusses, tall black-framed glass
walls, rattan armchairs) set in a large green garden, with a covered wooden deck strung with Edison
bulbs and wooden tables on the lawn under the trees, edged by red-tipped hedges (photos). Reviewers
call it the greenest spot in East Jakarta, half park and half forest, a hidden gem in an unexpected
place, quiet, cozy and warm, with friendly staff and a clean restroom (reviews 1, 2, 4, 5). People come
for real meals as well as coffee: the matcha latte, ayam penyet with tahu and tempe and a properly hot
sambal, pangsit kuah, and bebek keranjang (reviews 1, 3, 5). One reviewer found the food average, so
the page praises the place first and lets the dishes speak through what reviewers said (review 2).

| [MOCKUP] Solo weekday visitor | know there is a quiet indoor seat | read about the window tables in the brick-and-glass hall |

## Primary actions
1. Get directions ("Petunjuk Arah", Google Maps directions to the listing's place)
2. See what to eat (in-page: "Wajib dicoba" and "Menu andalan")
3. Contact: placeholder only, until the owner gives a WhatsApp number or Instagram

The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone (390 × 844).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, reservations, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (illustrations
and placeholders only; the listing has no owner photos), multiple pages, a phone or WhatsApp number
(none listed), English version.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Should the site spell the street and area "Pondok Ranggon" (the listing writes the street as "Pd. Rangon")?
2. Is it OK for the site to talk to visitors as "kamu" rather than "Anda"?
3. Is "come for one coffee, stay through lunch" ("Datang untuk satu kopi, lalu betah sampai makan siang") a fair picture of Rene?
4. Are you happy for the site to lean on being an East Jakarta place (reviewers call it the greenest spot in Jakarta Timur), without a slogan?
5. Are weekend families and groups at the lawn tables, and solo weekday visitors working or reading at the window tables, a fair picture of your guests?
6. Are the seating areas named well as "Taman", "Teras kayu" and "Ruang bata & kaca"? (If you use other names, tell us.)
7. Is it OK to mention the clean, nice-smelling restroom (from a review)?
8. Are these names and groups right: Matcha Latte under "Kopi & non-kopi"; Ayam Penyet, Pangsit Kuah and Bebek Keranjang under "Makanan berat"?
9. Is the iced coffee with milk called Es Kopi Susu Gula Aren, and do you also serve Americano, Cappuccino, Es Teh Leci, Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng and Nasi Ayam Bakar? (Cross out any you don't.)
10. Is the chocolate-and-cheese snack in the wire basket Pisang Goreng Coklat Keju (not Roti Bakar Coklat Keju), and is "Kerupuk dengan saus cocol" OK for the crackers with a dip until you send the real name?
11. Are these "from" prices close: coffee and non-coffee from Rp 25rb, mains from Rp 40rb, snacks from Rp 25rb?
12. Is it OK to show the Google rating (4.2 from 618 reviews)?
13. Would you like a WhatsApp number and an Instagram account on the site? If yes, which?
14. Can you send your own photos of the garden, the deck, the hall and the four dishes, to replace the illustrations?
