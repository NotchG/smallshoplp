## Personality
A brick-and-glass cafe set in a huge green garden that reviewers call the greenest spot in East
Jakarta, half park and half forest, and a hidden gem in an unexpected place (reviews 1, 4, 5).
Quiet and cozy, with a warm outdoor area dominated by nature (review 2). Inside: exposed red brick,
a steep gabled roof on black steel trusses, tall black-framed glass walls and rattan armchairs; outside:
a covered wooden deck strung with Edison bulbs, and wooden tables on the lawn under the trees,
edged by red-tipped hedges (photos).
- [MOCKUP] A weekend escape without leaving Jakarta Timur: you come for one coffee and stay through lunch.
- [MOCKUP] Quietly proud to be East Jakarta (one reviewer tagged it #jaktimpride, review 2), without making a slogan of it.
