## Personality
A Karo kedai kopi in Cawang, where people from North Sumatra's Karo and Batak communities gather, eat traditional food and talk for hours (reviews 1, 3, 4, 5). Regulars say it feels like home and reminds them of their hometown (reviews 1, 3). It's an open-front, no-frills place under a metal canopy, with lime-green walls, long wooden benches and plastic stools, and on busy nights the tables spill out along a tree-lined road (photos).
- One review compares it to a "bar" for the Batak community, meaning a regular hangout (reviews 3)
- It works as a traditional Karo food stop as well as a coffee shop (reviews 5)
