# PRD: Kedai Kopi Perjumpanta mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Kedai Kopi Perjumpanta's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their lime-green
wall, their name, a tall glass of bandrek on a lime saucer, their own "SEDIA" banner), and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All site copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"` are in Bahasa Indonesia
(brief, tone of voice). Menu names are spelled exactly as the shop writes them.

## The shop in one paragraph
A Karo kedai kopi in Cawang, Kramat Jati, Jakarta Timur, listed as a cafe and coffee shop
(type), open 24 hours every day (hours), at moderate prices (listing), rated 4.5 from 721
Google reviews (listing). Karo people from North Sumatra make up most of its visitors, with the
wider Batak community too. They gather here, eat traditional Karo food and talk for hours, and
regulars say it feels like home and reminds them of their hometown (reviews 1, 3, 4, 5). It is
an open-front, no-frills place under a metal canopy: lime-green walls, long wooden benches,
white-topped tables, plastic stools, speakers, fans and a TV. On busy nights the tables spill
out along a tree-lined road (photos). The cloth menu banner reads "SEDIA" over Indomie Rebus,
Indomie Goreng, Bubur Kacang Hijo, Bandrek Susu, Bandrek Susu Telor, Fanta Susu, Sprite Susu
and Badak (photos), and reviewers mention the Lontong Medan and the bandrek (review 2).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages.

## Open questions for the owner
Each becomes a line in `owner-checklist.md`.

1. Can we show your mobile number for WhatsApp and calls? (Please send it; the listing has none.)
2. Is the address right: No. 4, RT.4/RW.9, Cawang, Kramat Jati, Jakarta Timur 13630? Which street is it on?
3. Is "dekat Gedung ASABRI, Cawang" the right landmark for finding you?
4. Are you open 24 hours every day, holidays included, so the page can say "Buka 24 jam"?
5. Does "Perjumpanta" mean roughly "our meeting place" in Karo, and is "tempat kita berjumpa" fine as the line under your name?
6. May the page greet visitors with "Mejuah-juah!"?
7. Is "Rindu kampung? Duduk dulu, pesan bandrek, kita cerita." a good line for your kedai?
8. Is "Rumah kedua orang Karo di Jakarta" right for you, with "Siapa pun boleh mampir" so everyone feels welcome?
9. Do people come for news from home, to catch up with family friends, and to watch football together on the TV?
10. Do office workers from around Cawang come after work or late at night?
11. Is everything on your SEDIA banner still served (Indomie Rebus, Indomie Goreng, Bubur Kacang Hijo, Bandrek Susu, Bandrek Susu Telor, Fanta Susu, Sprite Susu, Badak), and is Lontong Medan on the menu?
12. Do you serve Kopi Hitam, Kopi Susu, Teh Manis and Teh Susu, and is your coffee from North Sumatra?
13. Which Karo dishes does your kitchen make? Do you sell Cimpa?
14. Are these example prices close: Bandrek Susu Rp 12rb, Bandrek Susu Telor Rp 15rb, Kopi Hitam Rp 8rb, Kopi Susu Rp 12rb, Teh Manis Rp 6rb, Teh Susu Rp 10rb, Fanta Susu / Sprite Susu Rp 12rb, Badak Rp 10rb, Lontong Medan Rp 18rb, Indomie Rp 12rb (Rp 15rb with egg), Bubur Kacang Hijo Rp 8rb, Cimpa Rp 5rb? (If not, what are they?)
15. Is the casual "kamu" and "kita" right, or would you prefer "Anda" for older regulars?
16. Is the thin red, black and white uis stripe OK as a small decoration, or would you rather leave it out (or name the uis it should follow)?
17. The food and drinks are drawings for now. Would you send photos of the kedai front, a glass of Bandrek Susu, a plate of Lontong Medan, your Karo dishes and a busy night (with permission from anyone recognisable)?
