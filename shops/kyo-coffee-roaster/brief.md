## Personality
A calm, Japanese-inspired coffee shop that takes its coffee seriously. Reviewers point to Japanese
stone gardens and temple or shrine ornaments, a clean, modern indoor room and a garden that is lit
up at night (reviews 1, 2). Relaxing music and hospitable, friendly staff (reviews 4, 5). Inside:
honey-coloured plywood panels laid out in a shoji-like grid, polished concrete floor and a concrete
bar, tall wood-framed windows onto greenery, dried flowers on the counter and a glass case of fresh
bakes (photos).
- [MOCKUP] Quietly playful under the calm: the kettle-with-eyes mascot on the cups suggests a brand that doesn't take itself too seriously.
- [MOCKUP] A morning stop as much as an evening one: it opens at 07:00 every day, so the page can pitch an early coffee and a pastry before work.
