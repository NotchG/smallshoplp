# PRD: Kyō Coffee Roaster mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Kyō Coffee Roaster's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

## The shop in one paragraph
Kyō Coffee Roaster is a calm, Japanese-inspired cafe on Jl. Wirajasa / Jl. Raya Jatiwaringin in
Cipinang Melayu, East Jakarta (listing). It opens every day at 07.00 and closes at 21.00 Monday to
Thursday and 22.00 Friday to Sunday (hours). Reviewers single out:
- the coffee: exceptional, with a wide list of manual brews, milk-based drinks and house blends with
  unusual flavours (reviews 2, 3, 4);
- the Marbled Cold Brew: strong, not watery, between black coffee and a latte (review 3);
- the bakes: pecan tart and pie (reviews 4, 5);
- the space: a clean Japanese-style room inspired by stone gardens and shrine ornaments, and a
  garden lit up at night (reviews 1, 2);
- the people and the sound: kind staff and relaxing music (reviews 4, 5).

People come to catch up with friends, study or just chill (reviews 1, 4). There are few power
outlets (review 1) and street parking is limited (review 4). Google rating: 4.6 from 1,328 reviews (listing).

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Local on the way to work or class | Check it's open now | See today's hours in the first screen and get directions in one tap |
| Friends planning an evening | Know the vibe and how late it's open | See the night garden and "Jumat–Minggu 07.00–22.00"; find the parking note |
| Coffee people (manual brew, black coffee) | Judge whether the coffee is serious | See the Marbled Cold Brew, the manual-brew list and the drink categories |
| Students looking for a place | See if it's calm and pleasant | See the room, the music and the "study, chat or chill" note; honest hours |
| Someone who found it on Maps or social | Confirm where it is and how to ask | See the address, map, phone and WhatsApp |

## Primary actions
1. Get directions ("Petunjuk Arah")  2. WhatsApp or call  3. See the menu (scroll to "Dari bar kami")
On a phone, the first screen shows action 1 without scrolling.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (illustrations and
placeholders only, since the owner has provided none), multiple pages.

## Open questions for the owner
Write these into `owner-checklist.md` in Bahasa Indonesia, in this order.
1. Is a calm tone with a light, dry wit right for Kyō (e.g. "Kopinya serius. Ruangnya tenang. Kamu tinggal duduk.")?
2. Is it right to pitch Kyō as a 07.00 coffee-and-pastry stop before work or class, as well as an evening spot?
3. Is the casual "kamu" OK, or would you prefer the more formal "Anda"?
4. Does 0812-1284-4322 take WhatsApp?
5. Does your manual-brew list rotate Indonesian single origins such as Gayo, Kerinci and Flores Bajawa? If not, which origins?
6. Do you serve a matcha latte and a hojicha latte?
7. Are these prices about right: manual brew from Rp 35rb, milk coffee from Rp 30rb, Marbled Cold Brew Rp 38rb, tarts and pastries from Rp 25rb?
8. Is "Tart apel" the right name for the caramelised apple tart? If not, what is it called on your menu?
9. Do you roast and sell beans to take home (e.g. 250 g bags)? If yes, we'll add a "Biji kopi untuk di rumah" section.
10. Is the section copy OK: the hero line, the "Ala Jepang" group name, and the menu and bakes intros?
11. Can you send your own photos (the bar, the garden at night, the bakes, the Marbled Cold Brew) to replace the illustrations?
12. May we use the kettle mascot from your cups? Until you say yes and send the file, we use a text wordmark.
