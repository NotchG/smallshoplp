A homey, cute cafe in Rawamangun that feels calm and set back from the street (reviews 1, 2, 3, 4). Every coffee
comes with a free cookie, and a little bear mascot turns up on the logo, the glasses, the paper liners and the cookies themselves (reviews 3, 4, 5, photos).
- Roomy, with several rooms and space for workshops and yoga sessions (reviews 5); stairs lead to an upper floor (photos)
- Music kept low enough to talk comfortably (reviews 5)
- Decorates for the seasons: gold "Kareem" balloons and paper lanterns for Ramadan, snowflakes and ribbon garlands for Christmas (photos)
