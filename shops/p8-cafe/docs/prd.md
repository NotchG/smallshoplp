# PRD: P8 Cafe mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes P8 Cafe's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (copy, buttons, hours, draft banner, `lang="id"`).
Register: "kamu" ([MOCKUP], owner may prefer "Anda").

## The shop in one paragraph
P8 Cafe is a homey, cute cafe at Jl. Pinang Raya No.8 in Rawamangun, Jakarta Timur, open every
day from 10.00 (to 21.00 Mon–Thu, 22.00 Fri–Sun). It is listed as a breakfast restaurant, cafe,
dessert shop and confectionery, so the page treats it as brunch and all-day. People come to work
because it is quiet and set back from street noise, to hang out with friends over music kept low
enough to talk, and to hold workshops and yoga sessions in its several rooms. Every coffee arrives
with a free bear-shaped butter or peanut cookie, which reviewers single out as delicious; the
signature iced coffee comes as frozen coffee balls in a tall P8 glass with a little jug of milk to
pour yourself, on a steel tray lined with P8 paper. Cupcakes and cakes sit in the "ORDER HERE" case
on a terracotta breeze-block counter, "P8 Butter Cookies" are sold by the jar, and Lebaran hampers
appear in season. Rated 4.7 from 229 Google reviews. No phone number or website in the listing.

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Someone looking for a quiet place to work (WFC) | know it's calm and open today | see "sudut tenang" and today's hours in the first screen; read "Kerja & ngumpul" |
| Friends planning a hangout | get the feel and the menu | see the free cookie, the ice-ball coffee and the menu groups within two scrolls |
| Workshop or yoga organiser | know there are rooms and how to ask | read "Ruang acara" and reach WhatsApp from it |
| Cookie or hamper buyer (esp. Lebaran) | see the jars and how to order | see the jar in "Bawa pulang" with a WhatsApp action beside it |
| First-time visitor on the way | find the place | tap "Petunjuk Arah" in the first screen; map and full hours in "Kunjungi kami" |

## Primary actions
1. Petunjuk Arah (Google Maps link from the listing)  2. WhatsApp (placeholder until the owner sends a number)  3. Telepon (placeholder)
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone (390 × ~750 visible).

Not on the page (brief says confirm first or don't mention): the "P8 = No.8" story, "old Rawamangun
house", parking, students/campus audience, coffee strength. They appear only as checklist questions.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is the hero line "Kopi, kue mentega, dan sudut tenang di Rawamangun" right for you?
2. Is it OK that the site speaks to visitors as "kamu"? (Or do you prefer "Anda"?)
3. What WhatsApp number should the WhatsApp buttons use? And which number for "Telepon"?
4. Is "Es Kopi Bola Es" OK as the name of the iced coffee with coffee ice balls? If not, what is it called on your menu?
5. Is Rp 35rb right for that iced coffee?
6. Do you serve kopi susu gula aren (Rp 28rb), matcha latte, iced lemon tea and iced chocolate?
7. Do you serve brunch plates like French toast with honey butter, scrambled eggs on sourdough, and a big breakfast plate with chicken sausage?
8. Are chicken katsu curry rice, beef teriyaki rice and nasi goreng P8 on the menu, and are rice plates about Rp 45rb?
9. Is the red velvet cupcake about Rp 25rb?
10. Is a jar of P8 Butter Cookies Rp 40rb, and do you sell original butter, peanut and chocolate chip flavours?
11. Is the Hampers Lebaran price still Rp 40.000? Should we show a price next season?
12. Can customers pre-order cookie jars and hampers by WhatsApp?
13. Can people ask about the rooms (workshops, yoga, arisan, birthdays) by WhatsApp? Are small gatherings like arisan and birthdays welcome?
14. Should the Ramadan bukber note (plating menu, Es Rujak Gembira) stay on the page all year?
15. May the site use your bear as a small recurring stamp? Can you send the logo and bear drawing file so we can replace our placeholder bear? Does the bear have a name?
16. Is it OK that the site borrows your breeze-block counter and P8 tray paper as patterns?
17. Does "P8" come from the address, Jl. Pinang Raya No.8? Would you like that story on the site?
18. Is the building an old Rawamangun house turned into a cafe? Would you like that mentioned?
19. Would you like a line about parking, e.g. "Parkir mobil terbatas, lebih mudah naik motor atau ojol"?
20. Do you have more photos of your space, food and cookies you'd like on the site?
