# PRD: Pintu Dua Coffee mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Pintu Dua Coffee's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

## The shop in one paragraph
Pintu Dua Coffee is an industrial-style coffee house with a rooftop, on Jl. Manunggal XVII in
Lubang Buaya, Cipayung (listing; reviews 3, 4). Downstairs there's a coffee bar with grey concrete
walls, wire pendant lamps and shelves of syrups and coffee bags. Upstairs is a covered rooftop under a
corrugated roof, strung with festoon lights (photos). It's open every day from 09:00 to midnight
(hours), with moderate prices and a 4.7 Google rating from 289 reviews (listing). People come with
friends or a partner to talk. They praise the cozy room, music kept low enough for conversation,
good food and drinks, and friendly, helpful baristas and staff (reviews 1, 2, 3, 5). The rooftop
stands out because few places in the suburbs have one (review 4), though it can get crowded.
The business's own photos show a biscuit-topped iced milky drink in a branded "Pintu Dua coffee &
kitchen" cup, a snack tray of fries, sausages and nuggets, and dimsum with a torched orange sauce
(owner-1, owner-2).

| [MOCKUP] Student or young worker nearby | find a daytime spot to work or study | see opening from 09.00 and one line about the indoor room by day |

## Primary actions
1. Petunjuk Arah (Google Maps directions)  2. WhatsApp  3. Telepon (call)
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out: a rooftop photo (none from the owner
yet) and live "open now" logic (the hours are the same every day, so the hero line is static).
