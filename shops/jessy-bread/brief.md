## Personality
An old Rawamangun favourite, loved by many people for good reason (review 4). It's a bread-and-cake shop with Indonesian and international food (description).
Two floors: the bakery downstairs, and a small restaurant upstairs where you can eat in, or you can take your bread away (reviews 3, 5). The shop is clean, the toilet is too, and the staff are friendly and helpful (review 1).
Outside: a grey two-storey front with orange trim, a paved forecourt for parking, and an oval "Jessy Bread & Cake" sign (photos). Inside, the cake case is lined with gold trim (photos).
