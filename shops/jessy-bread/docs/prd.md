# PRD: Jessy Bread mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Jessy Bread's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their own
charcoal-and-orange "Jessy / Bread" lettering from the bread bags, their gold-railed cake case,
their own price-card names and prices), and the checklist questions are few enough to answer
in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"`.
Cake and menu names stay exactly as the shop writes them (Tiramissu, Chocolate Mouse,
Jessy Delight, Double Choco Cake, Strawberry Cheese, Blackforest, Birthday Cake Large).

## The shop in one paragraph
Jessy Bread is an old Rawamangun bakery that many people love (review 4): a bread-and-cake shop
with Indonesian and international food, parking and wifi (description), open every day
06:00–22:00 (hours) and priced as inexpensive (listing). It has two floors: the bakery downstairs
and a small restaurant upstairs, where you can eat in or take your bread away (reviews 3, 5).
People come for a wide choice of bread and cake (reviews 2, 4, 5), prices lower than other
bakeries nearby (reviews 3, 4), birthday cakes (one family always buys theirs here, black forest
being the favourite, review 2), delicate chocolate and cheese cakes (review 1), always-fresh whole
grain and multigrain bread (review 2), traditional cakes and breads alongside red velvet and
rainbow cake (review 4). The shop and toilet are clean and the staff friendly (review 1).
Google rating 4.6 from 1,009 reviews (listing). Phone (021) 4896888, a landline (listing).

## Visitors and what they need

| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |

| Family planning a birthday (review 2) | see which cakes there are and what they cost | see the cake case with names, sizes and prices, and call the shop from the same section |

| Whole grain / multigrain bread buyer (review 2) | know they stock it | find whole grain and multigrain named in the bread section |
| Early riser, student, office worker [MOCKUP audience] | check it's open early | see "Buka setiap hari 06.00–22.00" in the first screen |
| Diner after a cheap meal (review 5, description) | know there's a restaurant upstairs | see the upstairs section: kind of food, eat in or take away, parking and wifi |
| Someone who has never been | find the shop | see the address, the map, what the building looks like, and a directions button |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (illustrations only:
all three listing photos are customer uploads and stay reference-only), multiple pages, a
WhatsApp button (the listed number is a landline), a second-outlet page.
