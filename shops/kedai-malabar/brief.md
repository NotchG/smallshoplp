## Personality
A neighbourhood "roti & kopi" kedai dressed like a small European bistro: red brick, white arched windows, black-and-white striped awnings and string lights over the terrace (photos). Easy-going and affordable, with indoor, outdoor, smoking and non-smoking seating, and a terrace that is at its best at night (reviews 1, 2, 3).
- Staff are friendly and helpful (review 3)
- Guests like the decoration (review 3)
