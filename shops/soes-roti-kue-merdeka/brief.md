## Personality
An old-school toko kue on Jalan Balai Pustaka: wood-grain cabinets, glass display cases with chrome
trim, white floor tiles, big glass jars of abon on the counter, framed black-and-white photos on the
wall and a stack of pale peach Merdeka boxes above the shelves (photos; review 4).
- [MOCKUP] The kind of shop people stop at before visiting family, to pick up a box of soes and a jar of abon.
- [MOCKUP] Quiet, tidy and unhurried, run like a family shop.
