# PRD: Have A Treat mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Have A Treat's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (`lang="id"`, buttons, hours, draft banner). Menu
names and the shop's English mottos stay as the shop writes them ("Have A Treat", "HP Potion",
"It's all good", "Better days are coming").

## The shop in one paragraph
Have A Treat is a small, cosy dessert-and-brunch cafe at Ruko The Boulevard No. 30, Jakarta
Garden City (Cakung, East Jakarta), open every day 10:00–22:00, moderately priced, rated 4.8
from 183 Google reviews. People call it cute and beautifully decorated: cream plaster walls,
arched windows and arched wooden shelves full of books, Edison bulbs hanging from planter boxes,
wavy black-and-white cushions, and its own wall mottos ("It's all good", "Better days are
coming"). Couples, friends and small families come to hang out; some bring a laptop. Reviewers
praise the tiramisu (balanced sweetness, bitter chocolate, whipped cream), the soft lemony
cheesecake, the french toast, a cappuccino one called the best in town, a matcha latte that
isn't artificial or too sweet, the HP Potion peach drink, the chicken wings, and an omelette
breakfast. The reviews are three to four years old, so the menu may have changed.

| Local in JGC / East Jakarta deciding where to go today | check it's open and how to get there | see "Buka hari ini 10.00–22.00" and "Petunjuk Arah" in the first phone screen |

| Small family looking for brunch | know there is real food, not only cake | see the omelette breakfast photo and chicken wings in "Sarapan & makan" |

| Visitor arriving from Instagram | call, message or find the place | tap call, Instagram and directions in "Hubungi kami" |

Copy tone [MOCKUP]: casual Jakarta-cafe Indonesian, addresses the reader as "kamu", upbeat, treats
as small rewards, never guilt. Do not headline the critical notes from review 1 (seasoning, peach
flavour, loud music).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: live open/closed status (hours are the
same every day, so the static "Buka hari ini" line is always true), star graphics, menu prices
beyond the three flagged example lines.
