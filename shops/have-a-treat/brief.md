## Personality
A small, cosy dessert-and-brunch cafe that people call cute and beautifully decorated: not too
big, comfortable, with an interesting menu (reviews 1, 3, 4). Cream plaster walls, arched
windows and arched wooden shelves full of books, Edison bulbs hanging from planter boxes, wavy
black-and-white cushions, and its own little mottos on the walls: "It's all good" in wire
script and "Better days are coming" on a letterboard (photos).
- [MOCKUP] A sweet break between errands: a cake and a coffee that turn into an hour.
- [MOCKUP] Upbeat and gently optimistic, taking its cue from the wall mottos.

## Audience
- Couples on a date, friends hanging out, small families (review 4)
- People bringing a laptop to work (review 2). One reviewer found the music too loud for work or meetings (review 1), so don't oversell it as a work cafe.
- Dessert lovers (reviews 1, 2, 3)
- People who care about a good-looking space (reviews 3, 4)

## Signature products
- Tiramisu: balanced sweetness, bitter chocolate, whipped cream, cute presentation (reviews 1, 2)
- Cheesecake, soft with a hint of lemon (review 3)
- French toast, which one reviewer rated the best (review 3)
- Cappuccino, called the best in town by one reviewer (review 5)
- Matcha latte that doesn't taste artificial and isn't too sweet (review 2)
- Chicken wings, the food tip one reviewer insists on (review 5)
- Omelette breakfast: omelette with mushrooms, sausages, bacon, sautéed potatoes with onion, and a baked tomato with spinach and cheese (review 1, owner-2)
- "HP Potion", a refreshing peach drink (review 1)
- A chilled cake display in the dining room (photos)
- Note: the listing's reviews are three to four years old, so any of these may have changed.
- [MOCKUP] A rotating "cake of the day" in the display next to the tiramisu and lemon cheesecake, e.g. a chocolate cake or a fruit tart.
- [MOCKUP] Breakfast plates served all day from opening (10:00).
- [MOCKUP] Example prices: coffee from Rp 30rb, cakes from Rp 35rb, breakfast and mains from Rp 60rb.

## What customers praise
- Cosy, beautiful, photogenic space; one reviewer called it the best-looking cafe in Jakarta (reviews 1, 3, 4)
- Desserts with the sweetness balanced just right (reviews 1, 2, 3)
- Fresh, delicious drinks (reviews 3, 4)
- Food as good as the drinks, which surprised people at a cafe (reviews 3, 5)
- An underrated find near JGC (review 2)
- Note: one reviewer found the omelette breakfast under-seasoned and a bit oily, the peach in HP Potion artificial, and the music loud (review 1). Don't headline those.

(photos). Talks about treats as small rewards, never as indulgence guilt.

## Visual direction
- Palette mood: warm cream plaster, honey and teak wood, soft amber bulb light, black-and-white wave pattern, small touches of lilac and pink flowers; the shopfront glows warm at night (photos)
