## Personality
A bright, tidy all-rounder cafe in Duren Sawit. You can pop in for a coffee on the way to work, sit down
for a proper meal, or book a room for a meeting. It's clean and well equipped, and the menu runs from
Western to Indonesian (reviews 1, 3, 5).
- Two moods in one place: a dark, polished indoor room with black marble-look tables and globe pendants, and an outdoor area under white fabric canopies with string lights and plants (photos; review 5)
- Thoughtful facilities: power sockets at the seats, a clean toilet, a small mushola and a conference room (reviews 1, 2, 3, 5)
- [MOCKUP] A go-to spot for the neighbourhood: order at the counter, then stay for a meal or a small event
