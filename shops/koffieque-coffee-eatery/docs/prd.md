# PRD: KoffieQue mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes KoffieQue's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for every word on the page (`lang="id"`), including buttons,
hours, the draft banner and alt text. Dish names stay exactly as written below until the owner
sends the real menu.

## The shop in one paragraph
KoffieQue Coffee & Eatery is a bright, tidy all-round cafe on Jl. Rawa Domba in Duren Sawit,
Jakarta Timur, open every day (listing, hours). Some people drop in for a coffee on the way to
work. Others sit down for a meal from a menu that runs from Western to Indonesian with fair
portions, or book the conference room for an activity (reviews 1, 5). Reviewers say the coffee is
good, varied and fairly priced for the area, and the food affordable and tasty (reviews 1, 2, 5).
They also praise the place as clean, bright and comfortable, with a power socket at every seat, a
clean toilet and a small mushola for two or three people (reviews 1, 2, 3). It has two moods:
a polished indoor room with black marble-look tables, wire globe pendants and black steel-framed
glass partitions, and an outdoor area under white fabric canopies with warm string lights and
plants (photos, review 5).

Never on the page: the Google rating or review count, reviewer names, quoted review text, service
or friendliness claims (review 4), the platter (review 5), "best coffee" style taste claims (review 3),
promises of long laptop stays (review 2), customer photos.

Copy register: keep copy pronoun-free where possible; where a pronoun is needed use "kamu" [MOCKUP].

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Can we add a WhatsApp number for questions and meeting-room bookings? Please send the number.
2. Is there a house number or landmark on Jl. Rawa Domba we should add for directions?
3. Is the burger in your photo called "Beef Cheese Burger", and is Rp 45rb about right?
4. Are these coffee names right: Es Kopi Susu KoffieQue, Kopi Susu Gula Aren, Americano, Cappuccino, Caramel Latte, Manual Brew V60?
5. Are these non-coffee drinks right: Matcha Latte, Red Velvet, Lychee Tea, Lemon Tea?
6. Are these Western dishes right: Beef Cheese Burger, Chicken Katsu with Rice, Spaghetti Aglio Olio, French Fries, Chicken Wings?
7. Are these Indonesian dishes right: Nasi Goreng Kampung, Mie Goreng Jawa, Ayam Geprek, Rice Bowl Ayam Teriyaki?
8. Are these pastries right: Chocolate Danish, Butter Croissant?
9. Are the example prices close: coffee Rp 22rb–32rb, rice bowl Rp 35rb, nasi goreng Rp 32rb, pastry Rp 18rb?
10. Guests have photographed an iced latte, a glazed chicken rice bowl and a swirl pastry. Are these the Es Kopi Susu KoffieQue, Rice Bowl Ayam Teriyaki and Chocolate Danish, or something else?
11. Is it fine to show illustrations of the iced coffee, rice bowl, nasi goreng and croissant until you send photos?
12. Can guests book the meeting room through WhatsApp?
13. Is there a time limit for working on a laptop? (Until you tell us, the site makes no promise about long stays.)
14. Should the site speak to guests as "kamu" (casual) rather than "Anda"?
15. Is "a go-to spot for the neighbourhood, for a coffee, a meal or a small event" a fair description of KoffieQue?
16. Can you send a larger copy of the burger photo, plus photos of the indoor room, the outdoor canopy, the meeting room and a few signature drinks?
