# PRD: Mabsooth Cafe & Resto mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Mabsooth Cafe & Resto's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"`.
Register "Anda" [MOCKUP]. Arabic script appears only inside the logo lockup.

## The shop in one paragraph
Mabsooth Cafe & Resto is an Arabian-style cafe and restaurant at Jl. Raya Condet No.6, Cililitan,
Kramat Jati (listing), listed as a coffee shop, cafe and food store (type). Pink and red Moroccan
lanterns hang under a vine canopy over an open-sided terrace with rattan chairs and patterned cement
tiles, next to an air-conditioned, glass-walled lounge with leather sofas (photos). People come for
the lamb shank, egg-topped rice bowls, iced lychee tea, tea poured from silver Arabic teapots,
coffee from the espresso bar, and shisha (photos, reviews 3). Reviewers call it cozy and comfortable,
say the food is good and the prices affordable, and mention friendly service (reviews 1, 2, 3, 5).
It is open every day from 08.00 until midnight, and until 01.00 on Friday and Saturday (hours), and
the terrace fills with families and groups of friends at night (photos). Google rating 4.7 from 240
reviews (listing).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: customer photos (photo-1 to photo-3, reference
only), quoted or attributed reviews, any rooftop claim, football-night content, an Instagram link.

## Open questions for the owner
Used on the page:
1. Boleh kami pakai kalimat "Teh, kopi, dan hidangan Timur Tengah di Condet" di bagian atas?
2. Apakah nomor 0819-4744-4493 bisa dihubungi lewat WhatsApp?
3. Apakah Anda menerima pesanan meja untuk rombongan (arisan, makan malam keluarga) lewat WhatsApp?
4. Apakah nama "Lamb Shank" sudah sesuai dengan nama di menu Anda?
5. Apakah nama "Rice bowl" dan "Lychee Tea" sesuai dengan nama di menu?
6. Apakah contoh harga ini mendekati harga asli: Lychee Tea Rp 25rb, rice bowl Rp 35rb, nasi kebuli kambing Rp 55rb, lamb shank Rp 110rb, teh Arab satu teko Rp 40rb, shisha mulai Rp 75rb?
7. Apakah nasi kebuli kambing, nasi mandhi ayam dan nasi briyani ada di menu?
8. Apakah hummus dengan pita, mutabbal dan falafel ada di menu?
9. Apakah roti maryam dengan kari kambing ada di menu?
10. Apakah shai adeni, teh mint, kopi Arab dengan kurma dan lemon mint ada di menu?
11. Apakah rasa shisha apel ganda, mint, anggur dan blueberry tersedia?
12. Boleh kami pakai sapaan "Ahlan wa sahlan. Silakan duduk, tehnya kami siapkan."?
13. Boleh kami pakai kalimat "Duduk di teras di bawah lentera, atau di lounge yang sejuk."?
