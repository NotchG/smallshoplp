## Personality
An Arabian-style cafe and restaurant on Jl. Raya Condet. Pink and red Moroccan lanterns hang under a vine canopy,
the floors are patterned tiles, silver teapots sit on the tables, and the terrace fills up at night (photos). Reviewers
call it cozy and comfortable, with friendly service and fair prices (reviews 1, 2, 3, 5).
- A late-night spot: open until midnight every day, and until 01:00 on Friday and Saturday (hours)
- Two moods: an open-sided terrace with rattan chairs, and an air-conditioned glass-walled lounge with leather sofas (photos)
- Shisha is part of the experience; one reviewer calls it the best hookah spot in Condet (reviews 3, photos)
- [MOCKUP] "Mabsooth" is Arabic for "happy, content". The page could play on that ("pulang dengan hati senang"), if the owner likes the idea
- [MOCKUP] Middle Eastern hospitality: tea poured for the whole table, long unhurried evenings with friends and family
