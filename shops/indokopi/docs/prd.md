# PRD: Indokopi mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Indokopi's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for all copy, buttons, hours and the draft banner, `lang="id"`.
Menu, promo and dish names stay exactly as the shop writes them (Caramel Latte, Matcha,
"Promo Bestie Berdua", "Promo Night Healing", "Workspace", "Berry Series", "Beli 3 Gratis 1").

## The shop in one paragraph
Indokopi is a small, lively neighbourhood coffee spot in Rawa Bunga, Jatinegara (Jakarta Timur),
ordered from a window counter, with seats beside the kiosk and out front by the road. The fascia
says "Coffee . Work . Thrift" and a yellow road-sign plate says "Kawasan Wajib Ngopi". It is open
09.00–03.00 Monday to Thursday and runs from Friday 09.00 straight through the weekend to
Sunday 24.00. Friends, "bestie" pairs, people who want to work from a cafe and night owls come
for iced milk coffees in can-shaped glasses on wooden coasters, matcha and caramel latte, Indomie
and promos like "Workspace" and "Night Healing". Customers praise the drinks, the fair prices,
the relaxed staff and how good it is for hanging out with friends (4.9 on Google from 430 reviews).

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Night owl deciding where to go tonight | know if it's open late today | see today's hours on the hero sign, first screen, no tap |
| Bestie pair looking for a cheap fun spot | see what's good and what's on promo | see illustrated favourites with prices, then the promo signs |
| Student or worker who wants to work there | know if it suits a laptop session | find the "Workspace" package and the Work pillar |
| Local or [MOCKUP] commuter passing through Jatinegara | get there | tap "Petunjuk Arah" in the first screen; see address and map |
| Matcha / latte fan | check the drinks people rave about | see Caramel Latte and Matcha drawn, named and priced |
| Someone with a question (group booking, promo) | ask quickly | tap "WhatsApp" in the first screen and again at the end |

## Primary actions
1. Petunjuk Arah (Google Maps link from the listing)  2. WhatsApp chat ([MOCKUP] that the number takes WhatsApp; "Telepon" as the fact-based fallback)  3. Read today's hours (no tap: it's on the hero sign)
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out: the banner's crowned-circle logo
(never reproduced; the name is set as a wordmark), customer photos (reference only), any
smoking imagery, testimonials as quotes, invented ratings, awards or staff names.

2. Is the weekend right: open Friday 09.00 non-stop until Sunday 24.00, then closed Monday 00.00–09.00? (Google shows Friday "9:00–24:00" and Saturday–Sunday "24 jam".)
3. Are all six promos still running: "Beli 3 Gratis 1", "Promo Bestie Berdua", "Promo Favorite Bestie", "Workspace", "Promo Night Healing", "Birthday Treat"?
4. 6. Is the menu list right (Kopi: Es Kopi Susu Indokopi, Caramel Latte, Hazelnut Latte, Americano, Kopi Tubruk; Non-kopi: Matcha Latte, Thai Tea, Red Velvet, Berry Series with Strawberry Yakult and Mixed Berry Soda; Makanan: Indomie Goreng/Rebus Telur, Pisang Bakar Coklat Keju, Kentang Goreng, Roti Bakar, Nasi Goreng)?

9. Do you have Wi-Fi and power outlets for laptop users?

11. Are these lines OK: "Kopi, teman cerita, di Jatinegara", "Ngopi dulu, cerita belakangan.", "Buka sampai jam 3 pagi. Weekend? Kami nggak tutup.", "Kerja siang, nongkrong malam, lihat-lihat thrift di sela-selanya.", "Balik lagi besok, ya."?
