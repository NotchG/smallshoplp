# PRD: Toko Roti Tegal mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Toko Roti Tegal's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their own bread bag,
"Tegal" in dark brown between orange scrolls), and the checklist questions are few enough to answer
in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (copy, buttons, hours, banner), `lang="id"`.

## The shop in one paragraph
Toko Roti Tegal is a long-running, old-fashioned bakery at Jl. Matraman Raya No. 68, Kebon Manggis,
Matraman, near Jalan Tegalan (listing, review 1). Customers call it nostalgic and "legendary": a long
wooden counter with glass cases of pastel and kue, buns sold one by one in the shop's own clear bags
printed "Toko Roti Tegal" with handwritten flavour stickers such as "kacang" and "cok ju", and shelves
of biscuit tins, syrups and gift boxes behind (reviews 1–3, photos). People love the chocolate bread
that tastes the same as in their childhood, the wide choice of Indonesian kue and roti, the
old-fashioned taste and the fair prices, and stop by for a snack to go with coffee or tea
(reviews 1, 2, 4, 5). Rated 4.4 from 305 Google reviews (listing). It is a take-away bakery; the page
does not promote eating in (review 3).

## Visitors and what they need

| Long-time regular who grew up on the bread (review 2) | check it's still the same shop and open today | recognise the bag and name instantly; see today's hours in the first screen |
| Passer-by on Matraman Raya after a snack for coffee or tea (review 1) | know if it's open now and how to get there | today's hours + "Petunjuk Arah" in the first screen on a phone |
| Shopper after Indonesian kue and roti at fair prices (reviews 4, 5) | see what's on the counter and roughly what it costs | see at least 3 roti and 3 kue drawn, named, with the example prices |
| [MOCKUP] Office worker buying kue for a meeting or arisan | find out if they can order ahead | read the kotak kue offer and call in one tap |
| [MOCKUP] Family ordering kotak kue for pengajian or a birthday | know notice needed and price from | "pesan sehari sebelumnya", "mulai Rp 20rb", call button |
| [MOCKUP] Holiday gift shopper (Lebaran, Natal, Imlek) | know if kue kering and gift tins are sold | see nastar, kastengel, putri salju in jars and gift boxes, then call |

## Primary actions
1. Petunjuk Arah (Google Maps)  2. Telepon (021) 8580766  3. Read today's hours
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone (390 × 844), together with action 2
and today's hours.
Once the owner gives a WhatsApp number, "Telepon" in the hero becomes "WhatsApp" (brief).

Not on the page, by rule: the founding year (only a reviewer's claim), any reviewer name, any quote
copied verbatim, "fresh daily" as a headline, dine-in or seating.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them; none yet, so all product visuals are flat illustrations),
multiple pages, a WhatsApp button before the owner gives a number, deployment.

## Open questions for the owner
Asked in Bahasa Indonesia; these become `owner-checklist.md`.
1. Apakah ada nomor WhatsApp untuk pesanan yang boleh kami tampilkan? (Kalau ada, tombol "Telepon" di bagian atas menjadi "WhatsApp".)
2. Apakah pesanan boleh lewat telepon di (021) 8580766?
3. Apakah kalimat "Roti dan kue rumahan di Jalan Matraman Raya." dan "Rasa yang sama, dari dulu sampai sekarang." cocok untuk toko Anda?
4. Apakah toko menjual roti keju, pisang cokelat, srikaya dan abon? (Roti cokelat, cok ju dan kacang sudah kami lihat.)
5. Apakah toko menjual risoles, lemper, kue lumpur, dadar gulung dan bolu pandan gulung? (Pastel sudah kami lihat.)
6. Apakah harga contoh ini benar: roti cokelat Rp 8rb, pastel Rp 6rb, kue basah mulai Rp 4rb, kotak kue mulai Rp 20rb? (Kalau tidak, kami ganti atau hapus.)
7. Apakah toko menerima pesanan kotak kue untuk acara (pengajian, ulang tahun, arisan, rapat kantor), dipesan sehari sebelumnya?
8. Apakah toko menjual kue kering (nastar, kastengel, putri salju) dalam toples dan kotak hadiah menjelang Lebaran, Natal dan Imlek?
9. Apakah sapaan "Anda" sudah pas, atau lebih suka "kamu"?
10. Bolehkah motif ukiran oranye dan tulisan "Tegal" dari kantong roti dipakai di situs, dan bisakah Anda mengirim file desainnya?
11. Bisakah Anda mengirim foto roti cokelat, tampak depan toko di Matraman Raya, dan etalase kaca tanpa orang? (Gambar produk di situs sekarang masih ilustrasi.)
12. Seorang pelanggan menulis toko ini sudah ada sejak 1968. Apakah itu benar, dan bolehkah tahunnya ditampilkan? (Belum kami tampilkan.)
