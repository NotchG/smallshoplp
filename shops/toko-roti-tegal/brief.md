## Personality
A long-running, old-fashioned bakery on Jalan Matraman Raya, near Jalan Tegalan, that people describe
as nostalgic and "legendary": a long wooden counter with glass cases of kue and pastel, bagged buns in
the shop's own printed bags, and shelves of tins and gift boxes behind (reviews 1, 2, 3; photos).
- Regulars say the chocolate bread tastes the same as it did in their childhood (review 2).
- [MOCKUP] A family-run toko that bakes the same recipes every day rather than chasing trends.

## Audience

## Signature products

- Indonesian kue and roti, "home bakery" style (reviews 2, 4, 5)
- Old-fashioned pastries (review 3)
