## Personality
A homey Indonesian kitchen and coffee spot in a converted house on a quiet residential street in Rawamangun. Reviewers call it a hidden gem: modest and cosy, with home-style cooking and a well-designed interior (reviews 1, 2, 4, 5).
- Tucked inside a residential area; two reviewers call it a hidden gem (reviews 1, 4)
- Mixes home-style Nusantara food, Chinese-style noodles and a pick-your-own warteg/ramesan-style counter (reviews 1, 3)
- Warm and welcoming from the moment you walk in (reviews 4)
- A small canvas at the entrance says "Home at last." in typewriter type (photos)
- [MOCKUP] The kind of place you come back to for the same plate every week: a neighbourhood kitchen more than a trendy cafe
- [MOCKUP] Recipes that feel like they come from a family kitchen (one reviewer says this; confirm with the owner before it appears as a claim)
