# PRD: Kedai Seroja mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Kedai Seroja's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (the red KEDAI SEROJA
sign as the headline), and the checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.
All site copy is in Bahasa Indonesia, with `lang="id"`.

## The shop in one paragraph
Kedai Seroja is a homey Indonesian kitchen and coffee spot in a converted house on a quiet residential
street in Rawamangun (Jl. Sawo No.15, Pulo Gadung). It serves home-style Nusantara food,
Chinese-style noodle dishes and a pick-your-own ramesan counter where guests build their own rice
plate, plus latte and other coffee (reviews 1–5). Reviewers single out Nasi Goreng Kecombrang,
Dendeng Batokok, Rendang, Bakwan Jagung, Lumpia and Risol Ragout. They praise the taste, the fair
prices for the quality, the coffee, a warm welcome and a cosy, well-designed interior with seating
inside and out. Two of them call it a hidden gem. It is open every day except Tuesday and rated
4.5 from 452 Google reviews (listing). Four of the five reviews are 2–3 years old, so menu claims stay
general and get confirmed.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out: Instagram or WhatsApp links (none known), and any
claim about family recipes, years in business or awards.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Should the site address visitors as "kamu" (relaxed)? If not, we switch to "Anda".
2. Is the tagline "Masakan rumahan & kopi di sudut Rawamangun" right for you?
3. Can we use the line "Masakan rumahan, di rumah yang hangat."?
4. Can we use the line "Pilih lauk sendiri, atau langsung pesan Nasi Goreng Kecombrang."?
5. Can we use the closing line "Mampir, ya. Atau telepon dulu."?
6. Is your noodle dish Bakmi Ayam Jamur, with a Bakmi Goreng option?
7. Are Ayam Bakar, Telur Balado, Tempe Orek, Sayur Lodeh, Perkedel and Sambal Goreng Ati usually in the ramesan counter?
8. Is the coffee menu Kopi Susu Gula Aren, Americano, Latte and Kopi Tubruk?
9. Are the non-coffee drinks Es Teh Manis, Es Jeruk and Teh Tarik?
10. Are these example prices close: Nasi Goreng Kecombrang Rp 35rb, Dendeng Batokok Rp 42rb, Nasi Rendang Rp 38rb, ramesan plate from Rp 22rb, Bakwan Jagung Rp 15rb, Risol Ragout Rp 8rb/pcs, Lumpia Rp 8rb/pcs, Donat Rp 10rb, Latte Rp 28rb, Kopi Susu Gula Aren Rp 25rb?
11. Are the short dish descriptions on the menu accurate?
12. Are Dendeng Batokok, Rendang, Bakwan Jagung, Lumpia, Risol Ragout and the donuts (meses cokelat, meses warna-warni, gula halus isi) still on the menu?
13. Are the opening hours still Mon and Wed–Thu 10.00–21.00, Fri–Sun 09.00–21.00, closed Tuesday?
14. Is there a landmark we should mention to help people find Jl. Sawo No.15?
15. Do you have a WhatsApp number to show next to (or instead of) the landline?
16. Do you have an Instagram account to link?
17. Is the "Home at last." canvas still at the entrance, and may we echo it on the site?
18. Would you describe Kedai Seroja as a neighbourhood kitchen people come back to every week, more than a trendy cafe? (Shapes the copy's tone.)
19. Are your recipes family recipes, and may we say so? (Not on the site until you say yes.)
20. Are students and lecturers from the nearby campuses among your regulars? (Copy only.)
21. Do families often come on weekend mornings? (Copy only.)
22. Can you send photos of the front with the red sign, the teak window and arches, the ramesan counter, Nasi Goreng Kecombrang and a latte?
