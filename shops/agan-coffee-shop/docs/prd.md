# PRD: Agan Coffee mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Agan Coffee's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Use "Agan Coffee" on the page, not the listing's "Agan Coffee Shop". The awning, the window and the cups all say "Agan Coffee" (brief). Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for all copy, buttons, hours and the draft banner, with `lang="id"`.

## The shop in one paragraph
Agan Coffee is a coffee shop in a glass-and-black-steel pavilion under a big shade tree at Jl. Angkasa No.13A, Halim Perdanakusuma, Jakarta Timur. It has a red scalloped awning, terracotta breeze-block walls and an exposed-brick room inside. It is open Monday to Saturday 08:00–23:00 and closed on Sunday. Prices are moderate, and guests call them affordable. It is rated 4.6 from 292 Google reviews. People come for good coffee (the Javanese coffee and the Americano get named), tasty meals and a friendly barista, and they either grab a quick cup or settle in for a few hours with friends or work. It has power sockets, several payment methods and indoor smoking. It is easy to spot from the road, and one guest called it a good stop after landing at Halim airport. Parking is narrow. The window lists Espresso, Cappuccino, Kaffee Latte and Macchiato. The owner's own photos show latte art in burgundy-rim cups, croissants, a pink blended drink in the branded cup, a fried cheese snack and dimsum.

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Traveller just off a flight at Halim | find a nearby place to sit and have a coffee | see whether it's open right now and tap directions, all in the first screen |
| Commuter on Jl. Angkasa | know if it's open and grab a cup | see today's status on the BUKA sign and get to the drinks list in one scroll |
| Friends meeting up / people bringing a laptop | check the vibe, the sockets, the prices | find the drink and food lists with example prices, and the "Nongkrong atau kerja" facts (sockets, payments) |
| Smoker, or someone avoiding smoke | know whether smoking is allowed indoors | find it stated plainly in "Kunjungi kami" |
| [MOCKUP] Office worker or resident nearby, early morning | know it opens at 08.00 and serves something to eat | see the 08.00 opening on the sign and the hours table, and croissants in the hero photo |
| The owner (the mockup's real audience) | recognise their shop | see their awning, their script name, their own photos, their real hours and address |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos beyond the owner's
seven uploads, multiple pages, a menu PDF, delivery-app links.
