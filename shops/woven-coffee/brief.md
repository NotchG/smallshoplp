## Personality
A modest, cozy neighbourhood coffee bar in Jatinegara: a pine-plank counter, shelves of books and brewing gear, amber pendant lamps, and a crew who know their coffee and enjoy sharing it (reviews 1, 2, 5; photos). It doesn't try to be fancy. It's a relaxed place to pop in for one drink or a brief chat with a friend (review 1).
- One guest was invited behind the bar to pull their own espresso, which made the visit feel like an experience (reviews 5)
