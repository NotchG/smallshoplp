# PRD: Woven Coffee mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Woven Coffee's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Recognition comes from things the shop already owns: the frosted "WOVEN COFFEE" band on the
glass doors, the boxed "KOPI ENA" bottle label, and their own photos (owner-1, owner-2).
All site copy is in Bahasa Indonesia (`lang="id"`).

## The shop in one paragraph
Woven Coffee is a modest, cozy neighbourhood coffee bar at Jl. Jatinegara Timur No.107,
Bali Mester, Jatinegara, close to the Kampung Melayu halte (listing; reviews 1). Inside there's
a pine-plank counter, shelves of books and brewing gear, and amber pendant lamps, with the name
frosted across the glass doors (photos). It serves a wide range of coffee (reviews 5): the house
signature Kopi Enak, also sold bottled with a "KOPI ENA – White" label (reviews 2; photos), and an
iced Vietnamese coffee a regular called their favourite (reviews 2). There's also a surprisingly good tea list served
in a white pot (reviews 1; owner-2), plus snacks and meals (reviews 3, 5). Friends come to talk,
office workers hang out after hours, people work on the Wi-Fi, and commuters drop in by bus or
motorbike (reviews 1, 2, 4). Guests praise the comfortable room, the tea, the signature coffee,
warm people who know their coffee, fair prices, the Wi-Fi, the air-conditioned smoking room and the easy
access. 4.6 from 302 Google reviews; open every day (listing; hours).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: live "open now" status beyond today's
hours, delivery-app links, a WhatsApp order form.
