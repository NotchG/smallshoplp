## Personality
A calm, carefully made hidden gem. It has industrial-modern rooms in warm wood and black steel, an
Indonesian-and-Western kitchen with standout fried rice, and many small considerate touches. Guests describe
it as more an experience than just a coffee stop (reviews 1, 3, 5; photos).
- Quiet on purpose: no background music, so the room is good for talking or working (reviews 2, 4)
- Considered details: a proper mushola with mukena laid out, very clean toilets with small bags for sanitary pads, and organised parking run by security (reviews 1, 3)
- Little surprises: a fish pond, a motorbike on display in the middle of the cafe, and an abaya shop inside (review 1)
- Several spaces: indoor rooms with AC, a ground-floor outdoor area, and an upstairs outdoor area where smoking is allowed (reviews 3, 4; photos)
- [MOCKUP] "Space" in the name reads as a promise: a place to work, gather, shop and pray, not just to drink coffee
