# PRD: Rumah Mantan Cafe & Nostalgia mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Rumah Mantan Cafe & Nostalgia's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their own
"Selamat Datang di Rumah Mantan" mural is the first thing on screen), and the checklist
questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"`.
Name on the page: "Rumah Mantan Cafe & Nostalgia" (listing); short name "Rumah Mantan".

## The shop in one paragraph
A cafe on Jl. Pinang Raya No. 10 in Rawamangun, Jakarta Timur, open every day (Sunday to Friday
11.30–21.30, Saturday from 14.30). Google calls it a cute, unique, creative hangout with unusual
snacks, food and drinks. The whole place plays on its name ("mantan" means "ex"): a sky-blue
"Selamat Datang di Rumah Mantan" mural with a little yellow house, and walls of hand-painted
signs about love and coffee ("KOPI: Ketika Otak Perlu Inspirasi", a striped "Kamu / Aku / … /
Sayang" sign, "stress free zone"). Pallet-wood tables and pine stools on the front terrace, a
glass-walled room inside, a smoking area. Young groups of friends come for low prices, a latte
praised for its taste, the carbonara and the nasi goreng spesial mantan, and say it got comfier
after a renovation. Rated 4.3 from 708 Google reviews; the listing's reviews are from 2017–2021.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: live-music listings, a full menu with
per-item prices.

## Open questions for the owner
Yes/no, one conversation. Becomes owner-checklist.md.
1. The striped "Kamu / Aku / … / Sayang" sign: we filled the unreadable word with "Pernah". OK? (And what does the real sign say?)
2. We redrew your entrance mural as the top of the page. Happy for it to be used like that?
3. Does 0812-9884-0096 take WhatsApp?
4. Is @rumahmantancafe_jkt still your Instagram?
5. Prices: coffee from Rp 15rb, snacks from Rp 15rb, mains from Rp 25rb. Roughly right?
6. Snacks: kentang goreng, pisang goreng, roti bakar and Indomie. All on the menu?
7. Drinks: es teh manis and kopi tubruk. Both on the menu?
8. "Roti Bakar Balikan" and "Es Kopi Susu Gagal Move On" are made-up names. Keep, or send us your real mantan-named dishes?
9. The black-bun hot dog: is "Hot dog roti hitam" OK, or what's its menu name?
10. The nasi goreng, carbonara and latte are drawings. Is the nasi goreng spesial mantan served with a fried egg like the drawing? Can you send real photos?
11. Is the latte still served on the rooster-pattern saucer?
12. The tone: casual "kamu/aku", laughing at exes with friends, never moping, plus the "Nostalgia" theme of old Indonesian things. Does that sound like you? (Covers the hero sign, the "Dindingnya penuh kode" paragraph, "Datang rame-rame? Ada tempatnya." and "Kali ini dijamin nggak canggung.")
13. Are students from the campuses around Rawamangun a big part of your crowd?
14. The renovation praise comes from 2019 reviews. Still fair to show "lebih nyaman sejak direnovasi"?
15. Is the smoking area still there, and are the terrace and glass room still set up the same way?
