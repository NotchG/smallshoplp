## Personality
A cheeky, nostalgic neighbourhood hangout built around its name ("mantan" means "ex"). Google
describes it as a cute, unique and creative place to hang out, with unusual snacks, food and drinks
(description). The walls are a joke book of love-and-ex signs: a "Selamat Datang di Rumah Mantan"
mural of a little yellow house, a "KOPI: Ketika Otak Perlu Inspirasi" sign, a hand-written
"Mantan" definition board, a striped "Kamu / Aku / … / Sayang" sign (one word unreadable), a love-story plaque, a "Marriage" chalkboard
and a "stress free zone" sign (photos). Rustic pallet-wood tables and stools on a front terrace, a
glass-walled room inside, a cashier counter of whitewashed planks, and a latte served on a retro
rooster-pattern saucer (photos). Cheap and comfy, and comfier since a renovation (reviews 4, 5).
- [MOCKUP] The "Nostalgia" in the name can carry the page: old Indonesian things (the rooster saucer, hand-painted signs) and old flames, both remembered fondly.
- [MOCKUP] It laughs at heartbreak rather than wallowing in it: a place to come with friends, not to cry alone.

## Tone of voice
Playful, cheeky and a little sentimental, full of wordplay about exes, moving on and the good old
days, like the signs on its walls. Never mean about anyone's ex.
- Site language: Bahasa Indonesia, for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"`
- Menu and dish names stay exactly as the shop uses them (nasi goreng spesial mantan) (review 1)
