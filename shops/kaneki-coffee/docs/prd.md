# PRD: Kaneki mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Kaneki's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Name on the page: "Kaneki", as on the glassware and gate sign. "coffee shop" is a descriptor
only ("Kaneki · coffee shop"), never part of the name. Site language: Bahasa Indonesia
(`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours and the draft banner.

## The shop in one paragraph
Kaneki is a small, calm coffee shop tucked into a residential neighbourhood on Jl. Perumkar DKI
in Pondok Kelapa, Duren Sawit, open every day 07.30–22.00. Inside: white walls, light-oak
counters, a large round paper lantern and a proper bar with an espresso machine, a big grinder
and pour-over drippers; outside: a concrete terrace under a bottle-green awning, where every
seat is a smoking area. Guests come for strong, flavourful coffee (many manual-brew options, a
house blend that works as an americano, unusual flavours such as charcoal), and for a butter
croissant that is crisp outside and soft inside. Matcha, peach tea, crispy truffle fries and
dimsum with mentai sauce round it out, and plant milk is available. Neighbours call it a hidden
gem close to home; remote workers come back again and again to work there. Google rating 4.5
from 646 reviews. (brief.md: listing, reviews 1–4, photos)

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages.

2. Is it right that you open at 07.30 every day and close at 22.00?
3. Is there a house number or landmark we should add so people can find you inside the housing area? (please send it)

5. Are these example prices close enough to show: Es kopi susu Rp 25rb, Americano Rp 22rb, Manual brew Rp 30rb, Charcoal latte Rp 30rb, Matcha latte Rp 30rb, Peach tea Rp 22rb, Butter croissant Rp 25rb, Dimsum mentai Rp 30rb, Truffle fries Rp 28rb? (please correct any)

7. Do you sell pain au chocolat and almond croissant?
8. Are Gayo, Kintamani and Flores Bajawa fair examples of beans for manual brew, and is V60 the method?
9. Is plant milk available for all milk drinks, or only some?
10. Is the hero line "Kopi seduh manual dan croissant hangat, dekat rumah." OK?
11. Is the other section copy OK (menu notes, "Seduh manual", "Suasana" panels, "Hubungi kami" line)?
12. Should the site speak as "kamu" (casual) rather than "Anda"?
13. Does "a quiet, crafted café with a short menu done carefully" describe you?
14. Is the light, slightly Japanese feel right, and does the name Kaneki have a story you'd like told?
15. Is the dark green of your awning the right accent colour?
16. Is a script similar to the one on your glasses OK as the wordmark until you send logo files? (please send the logo)
17. Are drawn illustrations of your drinks, food and space OK until you send photos? (please send photos of the bar, terrace, croissants and signature drinks)
18. Do morning commuters stop by for coffee on the way to work?
