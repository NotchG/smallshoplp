# PRD: Kopitro mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Kopitro's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Kopitro-specific test: the owner should see their own bar in the first screen: the black
"KOPITRO / MANUAL BREW" banner over the yellow-stained pine counter, with a V60 brew on it,
and tonight's opening hours.

## The shop in one paragraph
Kopitro is a cafe on Jl. Slamet Riyadi No.9-21, Kebon Manggis, Matraman, Jakarta Timur
(listing). It is a late-night manual-brew bar right by the road, open Tuesday to Sunday from
19:00 to 02:00 and closed on Mondays (hours). Under a black "KOPITRO / MANUAL BREW" banner, the
barista works at a yellow-stained pine counter with gooseneck kettles, clip-top bean jars and
a red hand grinder (photos). People come for the V60, which reviewers call top and which comes
in a portion that suits two, for coffee from several origins and brew methods, and for toast and
generous French fries (reviews 1, 3). The menu also shows a Vietnam drip over condensed milk and an
iced milk coffee in a Kopitro cup (photos). Friends meet there for coffee and music, and people
find it a cosy place for small talk. One reviewer liked the roadside atmosphere, with
vehicles going past (reviews 1, 2, 4). It is rated 4.7 on Google from 316 reviews (listing).
There is a "no alcohol" sign at the bar (photos).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out: a live "bean of the night" feed, a
music schedule, reservations, and any reproduction of other roasters' stickers.

## Open questions for the owner
Facts to confirm first:
1. Is Kopitro a fixed shop with its own space, rather than a street-side bar or stall? (decides the "Kunjungi kami" wording)
2. Do you still have live music on weekends?
3. Does 0815-1084-3658 take WhatsApp messages?
4. Can you send your logo file, so we can replace the red shape we drew behind "KOPITRO"?
5. Can you send a few photos of the bar, the drinks and the food, so we can replace the illustrations?

Menu and prices:
6. Is "Es Kopi Susu Kopitro" the right name for your iced milk coffee?
7. Is your toast called "Roti Bakar", and are cokelat keju and srikaya the right flavours?
8. Are your fries called "Kentang Goreng" on the menu?
9. Do you also brew Japanese iced (V60 iced), French press and Kopi Tubruk?
10. Are Gayo, Kintamani, Toraja and Flores Bajawa fair examples of the beans you serve?
11. Are these prices close: V60 Rp 25rb, V60 for two Rp 40rb, Vietnam Drip Rp 20rb, Es Kopi Susu Kopitro Rp 20rb, Kentang Goreng Rp 18rb, Roti Bakar Rp 18rb?

Copy and tone:
12. Is the hero line "Kopi seduh manual, buka sampai jam 2 pagi." right for you?
13. Is it fine to say the barista will suggest a bean if a guest can't choose?
14. Does "a proper cup after work, not a sugary one" describe Kopitro?
15. Are night workers, students and riders around Matraman a big part of your guests?
16. Is "kamu" the right way to address guests, or do you prefer "Anda"?
