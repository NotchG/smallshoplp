# PRD: SEATAP Kopitiam mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes SEATAP Kopitiam's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Recognition comes from three things the owner sees every day: the bottle-green wall, the
colour-block wall, and "Coffee · Eat · Friends". All site copy is in Bahasa Indonesia
(`lang="id"`), casual register "kamu" [MOCKUP].

## The shop in one paragraph
SEATAP Kopitiam is a cafe in a converted house in the Arundina area of Cibubur, Ciracas,
Jakarta Timur, open every day from 07.00 until 21.00 (22.00 on Friday and Saturday) (hours).
Inside it is bold and retro: a bottle-green wall with a round mirror, a wall of colour blocks,
crystal chandeliers, teak lounge chairs and green leather sofas, softened by palms (photos).
The sign sums it up as "Coffee · Eat · Friends" (photos). Regulars keep coming back (review 3),
friends meet over iced coffee and snacks (reviews 2, 4), and guests praise the crispy fried
cassava, the mung bean porridge, the friendly and fast staff, and fair prices (reviews 2–4).
Rated 4.5 on Google from 971 reviews (listing). Car parking is very limited (reviews 1, 4).

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Regular from Arundina / Cibubur, on a phone | check it's open today and go | see today's hours and "Petunjuk Arah" in the first screen |
| Friends planning a meetup | see if the place suits a group hangout | see the lounge photo in the first screen; reach WhatsApp in one tap |
| Early riser | know if breakfast is on before work | read "buka dari jam 7" in the hero and a breakfast section right after |
| First-timer | know what to order and roughly what it costs | see at least 6 products drawn, named and (where known) priced |
| Someone driving | know how to arrive | read a parking tip next to the map |

Not on the page: zuppa soup, anything about cleanliness, any promise of parking, customer
photos (photo-1/2/3), reviewer names, quotes, star widgets, the SEATAP Tea and Sky outlet
(until the owner asks for it).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: a second-outlet card, the owner's logo
file (we draw the wordmark), an Instagram link without a confirmed handle.

## Open questions for the owner
Becomes `owner-checklist.md`. Grouped so it fits one conversation.
1. Does 0811-953-156 take WhatsApp messages?
2. Should this page cover only the Arundina, Cibubur outlet? (If you want SEATAP Tea and Sky in Cipayung too, send its address and hours.) Is the "SEATAP Coffee and Space" Google account yours, and is that entrance photo this outlet?
3. Orders go through several counters: do all six "Menu andalan" items come from SEATAP's own counter?
4. Are these names right: "Es Kopi Susu", "Es Teh", and is the green layer cake a pandan cake (not matcha)?
5. Are these example prices close: Es Kopi Susu Rp 22rb, Singkong Goreng Rp 20rb, Pisang Goreng Rp 20rb, Bubur Kacang Hijau Rp 15rb, Roti Bakar Srikaya Rp 20rb, Telur Setengah Matang Rp 10rb, Kopi Tiam Rp 18rb, Nasi Lemak Rp 35rb? And what do the cake and Es Teh cost?
6. Is "Bundling paket" priced around Rp 42rb–48rb?
7. Do you serve a kopitiam breakfast from 07.00 (roti bakar srikaya, telur setengah matang, kopi tiam, nasi lemak)?
8. Is live music on every Friday? What time?
9. Is the parking tip ("parkir mobil terbatas, lebih mudah naik motor atau ojek online") OK to show?
10. Is "Bisa bayar tunai dan non-tunai" correct (e.g. QRIS, debit)?
11. Is "kamu" the right voice, or do you prefer "Anda"? Are the hero line and section lines OK, including "ada sudut sofa untuk ramean"?
12. Do you have an Instagram handle to link, and a logo file to replace our drawn wordmark?
