## Personality
A colourful, sociable kopitiam in a converted house in Cibubur, open from 07:00 for breakfast through to the evening (hours, review 1). Inside it's bold and retro: a bottle-green wall, a checkerboard wall of colour blocks, crystal chandeliers and teak lounge furniture, softened with lots of palms and plants (photos, review 2). The sign sums it up as "Coffee · Eat · Friends" (photos).
