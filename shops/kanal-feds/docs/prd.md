# PRD: Kanal Feds mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Kanal Feds's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours, the draft
banner and alt text. Running copy says "Kanal Feds"; the all-caps "KANAL FEDS" is the wordmark only.

## The shop in one paragraph
Kanal Feds is a carefully designed, compact neighbourhood coffee shop on Jl. Kolonel Sugiono No.45,
Duren Sawit, which reviewers place in Pondok Bambu (listing, reviews 3). It opens every day at 07.00
(Senin–Kamis until 21.00, Jumat–Minggu until 22.00), so people grab a takeaway coffee on the way to
work (hours, reviews 4). It sits right next to a gym, which makes it a stop after a workout (reviews 1).
It serves brunch, desserts, pastries, healthy drinks and food besides coffee, and great coffee
(reviews 2, 4, 5). The signature is "Sabor A Mi", a creamy coffee mocktail with a rich ube flavour
(reviews 2), and the cappuccino tastes unusually fruity and refreshing (reviews 3) and can be ordered
less sweet (reviews 5). People love the well-thought-out concept: a cool indoor room, a sofa corner,
an inviting outdoor area and a beautiful second floor with extra space and a prayer room, plus staff
who are genuinely warm (reviews 1, 2, 5). Google rating 4.7 from 228 reviews (listing).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also for this shop: no "work from cafe" pitch
(a reviewer found the tables too low for laptop work), no highlight of the shrimp dish, no claim about
beans, roaster or "specialty" coffee, no redrawing of the logo mark, no customer photos.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is "Kopi, brunch, dan teras hijau di Pondok Bambu" a good one-line description of Kanal Feds?
2. Does 0814-1053-8375 take WhatsApp messages?
3. Can customers order ahead by WhatsApp and pick up on the way to work?
4. Is it all right for the page to talk to people after the gym and mention healthy drinks and food (a light wellness angle)?
5. Do you want the page to name your coffee beans or roaster? (Right now it only says the cappuccino tastes fruity and refreshing.)
6. Does our illustration of Sabor A Mi (an ube cream layer over coffee in a tall glass) look like the real drink?
7. Is "Es kopi krim" the right name for the iced coffee with the thick cream layer? If not, what is it called?
8. Is "Cake mousse cokelat" the right name for the chocolate mousse cake with cocoa peaks?
9. Are these coffee drinks on your menu: es kopi susu, iced long black, cold brew, V60?
10. Are these non-coffee drinks on your menu: jus hijau (bayam, apel, nanas), smoothie pisang-oat, matcha latte, iced lemon tea?
11. Are these brunch plates on your menu: avocado toast dengan telur poached, rice bowl ayam sambal matah, nasi goreng udang, big breakfast plate?
12. A reviewer ordered a shrimp dish but forgot its name. Is it the nasi goreng udang? If not, what is it called?
13. Are these pastries on your menu: butter croissant, almond croissant, pain au chocolat, banana bread?
14. Are these example prices close: cappuccino Rp 32rb, Sabor A Mi Rp 42rb, es kopi susu Rp 28rb, jus hijau Rp 35rb, brunch Rp 55–75rb, cake mousse cokelat Rp 38rb, croissant Rp 25rb? (Please share your menu with prices.)
15. Is "kamu" the right way to address customers, or do you prefer "Anda"?
16. Are slow weekend mornings with families from Pondok Bambu and Duren Sawit a crowd you want the page to speak to?
17. Is the section copy (for example "Naik ke teras atas, duduk di bawah pohon, pesan Sabor A Mi.") in your voice?
18. Can you send the logo file, plus photos of the storefront, Sabor A Mi, the upstairs terrace, the indoor room, the sofa corner and two or three brunch plates? (The page shows labelled placeholders until then.)
19. Is it fine to say the shop is "di sebelah gym" (next to the gym) as a landmark?
