# PRD: Mardin Baklava Patisserie mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Mardin Baklava Patisserie's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for all copy, buttons, hours and the draft banner; `lang="id"`.
Turkish product names keep their spelling and sit in `<span lang="tr">`.

## The shop in one paragraph
A small, warm Turkish patisserie and dessert café on Jl. Cipinang Jaya Raya in Jatinegara,
East Jakarta, open every day (Fri from 13.00). Trays of baklava sit in a lit glass counter in
front of an exposed brick wall: square and carrot-slice pistachio baklava, pistachio and walnut
rolls, nests and chocolate baklava. The kunafe (crisp outside, melting cheese inside, syrup on
the side) is the dish people travel for, and Turkish tea in a tulip glass balances the sweets.
Customers praise the authentic Turkish taste, staff who explain every item, and a small,
clean, air-conditioned room. Sweetness divides people and reviewers find it pricey; parking is
limited. Rated 4.7 from 935 Google reviews. (brief.md: Basics, Personality, Signature products,
What customers praise.) Google lists it as "Buffet Restaurant"; the site presents it as a
patisserie and dessert café, as the brief directs.

| Kunafe seeker coming from further away (review 5) | confirm the kunafe and that the trip works | reach the Kunafe section in one scroll; find the address and the limited-parking note |
| First-timer worried about sweetness (reviews 3, 4, 5) | know what to order | read "Seberapa manis?" and find the less-sweet pick |

| Tea drinker (reviews 3, 5) | know they can sit down with tea | see the tea section and the note about the comfortable room |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages.
Also out: the story of the name (Mardin, the city) until the owner confirms it; saffron ice
cream (review 4 disliked it); any customer photo (photo-*.jpg); the Google category change
(a pitch note, not page content); ratings, awards, years in business or staff names beyond
the listing.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Do you take WhatsApp orders on 0821-1203-3331 for boxes and pre-orders?
2. May we use these Turkish names for the range: Fıstıklı Baklava, Havuç Dilimi, Fıstıklı Sarma, Cevizli Sarma, Bülbül Yuvası, Çikolatalı Baklava? (If you use other names, which?)
3. Are all six of these pieces on your regular menu?
4. Is your chocolate baklava the same as the chocolate pistachio baklava, and is it fine to recommend it as the less-sweet pick?
5. Is the kunafe still Rp 62,5rb, as on your chalkboard?
6. Is Rp 22rb–28rb per piece of baklava right?
7. Do you sell a mixed box of 6 at Rp 150rb and of 12 at Rp 285rb?
8. Can customers pre-order a 1 kg tray?
9. Do you take orders for Ramadan, Lebaran, hampers and office events?
10. Is Turkish tea (Çay) Rp 20rb and Turkish coffee (Türk Kahvesi) Rp 35rb, and do you serve Turkish coffee?
11. Is the section copy (headings and short intros) right for your shop, including the sweetness guide?
12. Should the page address visitors as "Anda" (rather than "kamu")?
13. Do you like the thin line ornament based on your gold serving platter?
14. Would you like the page to tell the story of the name Mardin (the city in south-east Turkey)?
15. Do you have other branches we should list?
16. Would you like help changing your Google category from "Buffet Restaurant" to "Pastry shop"?
