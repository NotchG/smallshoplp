# PRD: Al Buraq Cafe & Resto mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Al Buraq Cafe & Resto's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

## The shop in one paragraph
Al Buraq Cafe & Resto is a Middle-Eastern-style cafe and restaurant at Jl. Raya Condet No.37,
Batu Ampar, Kramat Jati, Jakarta Timur, open every day 15:00–24:00 (listing, hours). The room is
the draw: multicoloured stained-glass lanterns, deep red velvet sofas, arched wall niches and
Arabic music, plus a second room with slate-blue damask and blue-grey sofas where big groups sit
at long tables (photos, review 5). Regulars from Condet come for cozy, unhurried evenings, kind
and quick staff, and prices kept low to win long-term customers (reviews 1, 2, 5); you can
charge your phone if you bring a charger (review 1). On the table: a fried snack platter with
dips, grilled flatbread with a meat-and-fries dish in tomato sauce, iced tea with lemon and mint,
salted egg chicken, and foil trays of spiced rice with jugs of iced tea for groups (photos,
review 3). Shisha is the main draw in the reviews, but the brief keeps it to one optional line.
Google rating 4.3 from 465 reviews (listing). The praise dates from 2018–2020 and the newest
review criticised the food, so the page leads with the room, the service and group gatherings.

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Condet regular, late afternoon, on a phone | check it's open tonight and get there | see "Buka hari ini 15.00–24.00" and Petunjuk Arah in the first screen |
| Group organiser (friends, family, bukber, arisan) | know a big group fits and how to book | reach "Datang ramai-ramai" and a WhatsApp button that opens a prefilled message |
| Family or first-timer | see what the room and the food are like, and rough prices | see the room (Suasana), at least 3 dishes drawn, and a menu with example price ranges |
| Foreign visitor (reviews note few staff speak English) | find the place without asking | see the full address, a map and the directions link without reading much copy |

## Primary actions
1. Petunjuk Arah (Google Maps directions)  2. WhatsApp (ask or book a table)  3. Telepon
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: any shisha imagery or promotion beyond
the one hidden line, English or Arabic-script versions of the page, a live open/closed status.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Should the page spell the name "Al Buraq Cafe & Resto" (as on Google Maps) rather than "ALBURAQ CAFE & RESTO"?
2. Are the three photos on Google under "ALBURAQ CAFE & RESTO" yours, and may we use them on the site?
3. Does 0856-7855-946 take WhatsApp messages and table bookings?
4. Is "Lentera, sofa merah, dan teh hangat di Jalan Raya Condet" a good line for the top of the page?
5. Are you happy with the greeting "Ahlan wa sahlan!" on the page?
6. Is the casual "kamu" right for your guests, rather than "Anda"?
7. Is "Piring gorengan" the right name for the fried snack platter (risoles, sambosa, two dips)? If not, what do you call it?
8. Is "Roti panggang & daging saus tomat" the right name for the flatbread with the meat-and-fries bowl?
9. Is "Es teh lemon mint" the right name for the iced tea with lemon and mint?
10. Are "Nampan nasi rombongan" and "Teko es teh" the right names for the group rice tray and the iced-tea jug?
11. Do you serve Nasi Kebuli Kambing, Nasi Mandi Ayam and Nasi Briyani?
12. Do you serve Roti Maryam with kari kambing, Sambosa, and Hummus with roti?
13. Do you serve Teh Susu Arab, Kopi Arab, Es Lemon Tea Mint and Jus Alpukat?
14. Is Salted Egg Chicken still on the menu?
15. Are these price ranges close: snacks Rp 25rb–40rb, rice dishes Rp 45rb–75rb, drinks Rp 10rb–25rb?
16. Do you offer a group package for 5–10 people from about Rp 350rb, ordered a day ahead by WhatsApp?
17. Do you take bookings for buka bersama, arisan or office groups in the second room?
18. Is "groups come after Maghrib and stay until closing" a fair picture of your evenings?
19. Should the page mention shisha at all, with one line "Tersedia shisha (khusus 18+)"? (The human reviewer decides first; tobacco promotion is restricted.)
20. Is the room, menu and service still as reviewers described them in 2018–2020, including phone charging?
21. Is "Sampai ketemu malam ini" a good closing line?
