A Middle-Eastern-style lounge on Jalan Raya Condet: multicoloured stained-glass Moroccan lanterns, deep red velvet sofas, arched wall niches, Arabic music playing (photos, review 5). Staff are kind, helpful and quick to take orders, and prices are kept low to win regulars (reviews 1, 2).
- Cozy and comfortable for a long evening (reviews 1, 2)
- Easy to charge your phone, if you bring your own charger (review 1)
- Hosts big group gatherings at long tables, decorated with crescent-moon and star ornaments (photos)
