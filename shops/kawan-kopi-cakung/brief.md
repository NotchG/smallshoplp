## Personality
A small, carefully run coffee bar in a shophouse where the coffee is the point and the people
behind the bar are warm: the barista explains what's in the cup, and when an order went to the
wrong table they put it right and added a free coffee (reviews 1, 2). A bar clad in grey-green
glazed tiles, warm wood panelling, backlit ceiling panels, a grey tiled floor, wooden stools,
and low afternoon sun across the wood tables (photos).
- [MOCKUP] The name as the promise: your coffee friend ("kawan") down the street. Come alone, leave on first-name terms with the bar.
