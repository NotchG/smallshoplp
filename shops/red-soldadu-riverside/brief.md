## Personality
A quiet, green hideaway on the bank of the Ciliwung, tucked into a housing complex, where people come to slow down over coffee, mocktails and snacks to the sound of the river and the trees (reviews 1, 2, 3, 5). Reviewers keep calling it a hidden find (reviews 1, 2, 3, 5).
- Built as an open timber-and-steel pavilion with decks looking over the river (photos)
- Care in the small things: cutlery, glassware and tableware are chosen with an eye for detail (review 4)
- Clean, well designed, with a good overall vibe (review 5)
- A mushola sits nearby in the greenery, and reviewers mention it is clean and smells pleasant (reviews 1, 4)
- [MOCKUP] A place that rewards staying a while: laptop in the morning, mocktail as the light goes over the river.
