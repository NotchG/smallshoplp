# PRD: Red Soldadu Riverside mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Red Soldadu Riverside's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours and the draft
banner. Menu names stay as the shop writes them (English where the shop uses English).

## The shop in one paragraph
Red Soldadu Riverside is a coffee shop (also listed as cafe and food store) at Jl. Sederhana I
No.4A, Gedong, Pasar Rebo, Jakarta Timur. It sits inside a housing complex on a bend of the
Ciliwung river; reviewers place it in Cijantung. It's an open timber-and-steel pavilion with decks
over the river, open every day 08:00–23:00, rated 4.5 from 506 Google reviews. Visitors call it a
hidden find: quiet, green, with a river view they say is rare in East Jakarta. They come to
unwind, to work for a few hours, or for a relaxing day out. The things people order are the
coffee (the "Black and Berries" in particular), the Banana Fritter, the Cheese Baked Peuyeum,
Honey Wings and mocktails that surprised reviewers. They also notice the carefully chosen
cutlery and glassware, the cleanliness, and a clean mushola nearby. Mains are few and got mixed
comments, and prices are seen as a little high but worth it. (All from brief.md.)

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. No customer photos (`photo-*.jpg`) on the site.
No stock photos.

## Open questions for the owner
Grouped so they fit one conversation. Every one becomes a line in `owner-checklist.md`.

**Name and contact**
1. Is "Red Soldadu Riverside" (three words) the spelling you want, rather than "Redsoldadu"?
2. Is Riverside your only outlet? (If there are other Red Soldadu outlets, should the site mention them?)
3. Does 0812-1506-6612 take WhatsApp messages?
4. Are the hours still 08.00–23.00 every day?

**Voice**
5. Is the hero line "Kopi, mocktail, dan suara sungai di tepi Ciliwung" right for you?
6. Is talking to visitors as "kamu" OK, or do you prefer "Anda"?
7. Is "datang pagi bawa laptop, pulang setelah mocktail terakhir" a fair picture of your day? Are couples and small groups in the evening a big part of your crowd?

**Menu** (the reviews are 1–4 years old)
8. Are Black and Berries, Banana Fritter, Cheese Baked Peuyeum, Honey Wings, Iced Cappuccino, Iced Lychee, Beef Black Pepper and Spaghetti Bolognese still on the menu, spelled like this?
9. Is the coffee list right (Espresso, Americano, Cappuccino, Caffe Latte, Es Kopi Susu Gula Aren, V60 manual brew)?
10. Are the mocktails right (Lychee Mint Sparkling, Berry Lime Cooler, Passion Fruit Soda)? If not, what are yours called?
11. Are Kentang Goreng, Cireng and Roti Bakar on the menu?
12. Can we replace all example prices with your real ones? (Every price on the mockup is an example.)
13. Do you sell your coffee beans by the bag, and is "house blend 200 g" right?
14. Do the drawings of the five dishes look close enough? Is the plated dessert with almond flakes and berry sauce the Banana Fritter or the Cheese Baked Peuyeum?

**Place**
15. Do you have Wi-Fi and power sockets for people who work there?
16. Is parking still about three cars plus motorbikes?
17. Can you send your own photos (the deck, the river view, the bar, drinks and dishes) to replace the drawings?
