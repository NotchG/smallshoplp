## Personality
A cheap-and-cheerful neighbourhood coffee stop that's half modern iced-latte bar and half warung kopi. It
has an open yard under a tree, string lights, a steel kiosk and late hours (photos; hours; reviews 1, 4).
- Coffee at low prices is the whole idea: the cups say "Low Price High Taste" and the iced lattes are labelled Rp 15.000 (photos, owner-4)
- A real warkop counter: the kiosk's back wall is covered in strips of instant-drink sachets (photos, owner-1)
- Friendly people behind the counter; one reviewer named the kindness of the owner and staff (review 1)
- A place that comes alive at night, with warm bulbs strung over the seating (photos, owner-1, owner-3)
- [MOCKUP] The kind of spot where you order a kopi susu, then stay for Indomie and another round with friends
