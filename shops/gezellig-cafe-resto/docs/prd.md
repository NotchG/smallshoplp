# PRD: Gezellig Cafe & Resto mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Gezellig Cafe & Resto's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All site copy is in Bahasa Indonesia (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours and the draft banner.
Dish names stay exactly as the shop uses them.

## The shop in one paragraph
Gezellig Cafe & Resto is a cafe and restaurant at Jl. Pemuda No.74, Pulo Gadung, Jakarta Timur,
open every day 09:00–23:00 at a moderate price level, rated 4.7 from 361 Google reviews (listing,
hours). It is styled like a Dutch house: guests describe the indoor rooms as feeling like an old
house, with a beautiful chandelier, Holland ornaments, paintings, tulips and a miniature windmill
(reviews 3, 4). The kitchen serves Dutch dishes and Indonesian classics side by side: the Gezellig
Kofta Joppie, the Uitsmijter big breakfast, Garang Asem, Spaghetti Arrabbiata and fried banana
(reviews 1, 2, 4). Old friends meet up here, families and friend groups hang out, couples celebrate
birthdays, and people come for lunch (reviews 1, 2, 4, 5). Guests praise the food, the friendly,
quick service, the value, the spacious parking, the live music, and the split between a non-smoking
indoor room and an outdoor smoking area that is part open-air, part roofed (reviews 1–5).

## Visitors and what they need

| Couple planning a birthday or romantic dinner | judge the atmosphere and book a table | see the lamp-lit interior idea, the "Rayakan di sini" section and a WhatsApp button |

| Guest curious about Dutch dishes | understand what Kofta Joppie or Uitsmijter is | read a one-line description under every featured dish |

## Primary actions
1. Get directions ("Petunjuk Arah")  2. Message on WhatsApp ([MOCKUP] number takes WhatsApp) or call  3. Look at the menu

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. The owner provided no photos: every food and
room picture is a flat illustration or a labelled frame waiting for the owner's photo.
No Instagram link until the owner gives one.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Can guests reach you on WhatsApp at 0851-5515-9411?
2. Is "Hidangan Belanda & Nusantara dalam suasana rumah tua yang hangat" a good one-line description of Gezellig?
3. Is it right to explain the name as the Dutch word for a warm, cosy, together feeling, and is that why you chose it? (Tell us your own story if it's different.)
4. Is it right to call your kitchen Dutch-Indonesian, where Dutch home dishes meet Javanese cooking?
5. Is the beef patty with rice, sauce and red-cabbage salad in guests' photos the Gezellig Kofta Joppie? (Our drawing is based on it.)
6. Are Bitterballen and Poffertjes on your menu?
7. Are Bistik Jawa and Nasi Goreng Gezellig on your menu, under those names?
8. Are Kofta Joppie, Uitsmijter, Garang Asem, Spaghetti Arrabbiata and Pisang Goreng all still on the menu? (The reviews we used are 10–18 months old.)
9. Are these example prices close: Kofta Joppie Rp 58rb, Uitsmijter Rp 55rb, Garang Asem Rp 52rb, Spaghetti Arrabbiata Rp 48rb, Bitterballen Rp 35rb, Poffertjes Rp 32rb, Pisang Goreng Rp 25rb, Koffie Gezellig Rp 32rb, Strawberry Mojito Rp 30rb, Es Teh Manis Rp 15rb?
10. Do you serve a coffee called Koffie Gezellig? A guest described a rum-flavoured coffee: is it alcohol-free, and may we mention "rum" on the page? (We left the word out until you confirm.)
11. Are Strawberry Mojito (non-alkohol) and Es Teh Manis the right names for those drinks?
12. Is there live music on Friday and Saturday nights? If not, when?
13. Can guests book tables and small private events (birthdays, arisan) through WhatsApp?
14. Do you prefer addressing guests as "Anda" (as on the page) or "kamu"?
15. Do you have an Instagram account we should link?
16. Can you send your own photos to replace the drawings and empty frames: the night exterior with the black-framed doors, the chandelier room, the windmill and tulip corner, and the Kofta Joppie, Uitsmijter and Garang Asem?
17. Is the map pin in the right place?
