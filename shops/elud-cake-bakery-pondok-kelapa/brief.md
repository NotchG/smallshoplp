## Personality
A bright, well-stocked neighbourhood bakery that leads with value: its signboard calls it a birthday-cake
centre ("Pusat Kue Ulang Tahun") and a shop for all kinds of cakes and discounted bread ("Toko Aneka Kue &
Roti Diskon") (photos). Inside, trays of bagged buns sit under red-and-white hanging section signs
(Croissant, Roll, Roti Manis, Blackforest, Kasir), boxed brownies line the glass counter, and a wall of
fridges holds the birthday cakes and tarts (photos). A reviewer singles out the cheese bread and the
affordable prices (review 2).
