# PRD: Elud Cake & Bakery Pondok Kelapa mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Elud Cake & Bakery's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours and the draft
banner. Menu names stay exactly as the shop writes them (Croissant, Roll, Blackforest, Roti Smoke
Beef, Banana Crumble, Salt Bread, Soft Sourdough, Strudel, Pizza Cinta, Brownies Bakar Spesial).

## The shop in one paragraph
Elud Cake & Bakery is a bright neighbourhood bakery at Jl. Pondok Kelapa VI No. 10 in Duren Sawit,
Jakarta Timur, open every day 06.15–22.00. Its signs call it a birthday-cake centre ("Pusat Kue
Ulang Tahun & Tart") and a shop for all kinds of cakes and discounted bread ("Toko Aneka Kue & Roti
Diskon"). Inside, bagged buns (Roti Smoke Beef, Roti Banana Crumble, Roti Cokelat) sit on trays
under red-and-white section signs (Croissant, Roll, Roti Manis, Blackforest, Kasir), Brownies Bakar
Spesial in yellow boxes line the counter, and a wall of fridges holds whipped-cream birthday tarts
with chocolate drips, chocolate fans and cherries. A window banner announces new soft sourdough and
salt bread; there is a Strudel sign and heart-shaped "Pizza Cinta" in the window. Photo price tags
show markdowns (Rp 9.000 from Rp 15.000). One reviewer singles out the cheese bread and the
affordable prices. Google rating 4.7 from 234 reviews; all five fetched reviews are one line or less.

| Family buying a birthday cake (signage) | know Elud does birthday cakes and how to order | see the tarts and a call-to-order button in one section |
| [MOCKUP] Commuter or school-run parent | check it's open early | read "Buka setiap hari 06.15–22.00" in the first screen |
| [MOCKUP] Late shopper after a forgotten cake | check it's still open | same hours line; call button one tap away |
| Someone driving past or new to the area | find the shop | directions button in the first screen, map and full address lower down |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages, live "open now" status.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is this page for the Pondok Kelapa outlet only (is Elud a small chain with other branches)?
2. Are these prices from your tags still current: Roti Smoke Beef and Roti Banana Crumble Rp 9.000 (from Rp 15.000), Salt Bread Original Rp 12.000 (from Rp 15.000), Soft Sourdough Rp 15.000 (from Rp 20.000)?
3. Is the cheese bread customers praise sold as "Roti Keju"? (If not, what's its name on the tag?)
4. Is Pizza Cinta your own product?
5. Do you sell roti sobek, roti abon, bolu gulung pandan & keju and kue sus?
6. Are these example prices close: roti manis from Rp 8.000, Brownies Bakar Spesial Rp 55.000 per box, birthday tarts from Rp 150.000?
7. Can customers order a birthday cake with a name written on it by phone?
8. Is a birthday cake usually ready to take home the same day?
9. Does 0813-1130-8866 take WhatsApp orders?
10. What is your Instagram handle (the sign shows "@elud…")?
11. Is the line "Sudah buka sebelum jam sekolah, masih buka setelah makan malam" right for your customers?
12. Is "Selalu ada yang lagi diskon di nampan" (there's always a deal on the tray) true most days?
13. Should the site speak to customers as "kamu" (casual), or would you prefer "Anda"?
14. Is the short section copy (intro lines under each heading) okay as written?
15. Can you send photos of your bread, cakes and shop to replace the illustrations?
