## Personality
A bright, well-stocked cake-and-bread shop with a big chocolate-brown facade, chilled glass cases full
of decorated cakes, and a brand that leans into promos and celebrations: the opening day had balloons,
flower boards, a mascot cut-out and costumed characters out front (photos; review 1).
- [MOCKUP] The neighbourhood's last-minute birthday-cake stop: open until 22.00, so a whole cake is
  still in the case after dinner (hours).
- [MOCKUP] Cheerful and family-friendly rather than artisanal or fancy.
