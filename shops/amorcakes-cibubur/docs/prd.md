# PRD: Amorcakes & Bakery Cibubur mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes the owner of Amorcakes & Bakery Cibubur say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

## The shop in one paragraph
Amorcakes & Bakery Cibubur is a cake-and-bread shop on Jl. Lapangan Tembak / Jl. Belimbing No. 6
in Cibubur, Ciracas, Jakarta Timur. It is open every day 06.00–22.00 (hours) and part of
"Amor Group" (photos). The chocolate-brown facade carries a cream "amor cakes & bakery" sign. Inside,
chilled glass cases with white shelves hold birthday cakes: Black Forest with cherries and a
blank white plaque, chocolate-drip cakes, a pale-green cake with cherries and a chocolate fan,
red-glazed cream cakes in clear "mika" domes, roll cakes with round orange stickers, and boxed
"Boluamor" and "Snow Bolu". The opening banner also listed Roti Manis, Roti Tawar Mini, Doughnut
and Bolu (photos). It is rated 4.9 from 557 Google reviews. Customers mention the discounts and
promos and the friendly service (reviews 1, 2). The site is in Bahasa Indonesia, `lang="id"`.

## Out of scope
Online ordering or payment (the composer only opens WhatsApp with a prefilled text), booking
backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (there are no owner photos; photo-1/2/3 are customer
uploads and never appear), multiple pages, live "open now" status, any promo offer (the 2022
banner offers expired), the "Outlet kami" section (pending the owner's answer), and the Amor Group's
other outlets.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Does 0896-5499-3538 take WhatsApp orders?
2. Are you the person who decides on this outlet's website (rather than Amor Group)?
3. Should the page cover only the Cibubur outlet? (If not: should we add an "Outlet kami" section?)
4. Are Instagram/Facebook @boluamor and TikTok @amorcakes the right accounts for this outlet?
5. Is "cheerful, family-friendly, the neighbourhood's last-minute birthday-cake stop" the right feel?
6. Are Black Forest, Chocolate Drip, Green Tea, Strawberry Cream and Tiramisu flavours you sell?
7. Is the pale-green cake Green Tea, and is the red-glazed mika cake Strawberry Cream?
8. Do you write a name on the cake plaque for free?
9. Are your birthday-cake sizes 12, 16 and 20 cm?
10. Is "Deluxe 16" still Rp 130rb and "Deluxe 12 Cream (Mika)" still Rp 49rb?
11. Do whole cakes in the case go home the same day ("siap bawa hari ini juga")?
12. Are these prices right: Roti Manis from Rp 8rb, Doughnut Rp 7rb, Roti Tawar Mini Rp 12rb, Bolu gulung Rp 45rb?
13. Are the Roti Manis flavours cokelat, keju, srikaya, abon sapi and sosis?
14. Are the Bolu gulung flavours cokelat, pandan and keju?
15. Is bread ready from 06.00 ("Roti hangat dari jam 6 pagi")?
16. Do you take orders for offices, schools and arisan?
17. Is "Bolu dalam kotak, pas untuk oleh-oleh dan arisan" a fair line for Boluamor and Snow Bolu?
18. Are promos usually announced on Instagram?
19. Is "kamu" the right way to address customers (rather than "Anda")?
20. Is the hero line "Kue ulang tahun, bolu dan roti manis, buka dari pagi sampai malam" fine?
21. Is "Selamat Ulang Tahun" fine as the example plaque text?
22. Can you send the logo file, the opening-day facade photo (if it's yours), and fresh photos of the cake case and bread shelf? (Illustrations stand in until then.)
