## Personality
A custom birthday-cake maker in a Duren Sawit side lane, known for tall, very detailed theme cakes with
hand-modelled fondant figures, a friendly seller and low prices (reviews 4, 5; photos). Will still take an
order the day before and get it right (review 5). Every cake in the owner's photos is made for one child's
party: their name, their age, their favourite thing (photos).
- [MOCKUP] An order-first home cake studio rather than a walk-in shop: you send your theme on WhatsApp, then pick the cake up. The gang address, the "Kitchen" name and the order-led reviews point that way; the owner should confirm whether walk-in buyers can find ready cakes.
- [MOCKUP] Proud of the small details (a cat's whiskers, a mermaid's shells) and happy to build a cake around whatever the child loves.
