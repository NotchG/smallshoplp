# PRD: Bonjur Cafe & Resto mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Bonjur Cafe & Resto's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (copy, buttons, hours, draft banner, `lang="id"`).
Dish names stay as the shop writes them (Beef Teriyaki).

## The shop in one paragraph
Bonjur Cafe & Resto is a family cafe-and-resto on a house plot in Komp. Billy Moon, Pondok
Kelapa, Jakarta Timur, open every day from 07:00 until 22:00 (23:00 on Saturday and Sunday)
(hours). It has a glass-walled dining room with a green wall, crystal chandeliers and walnut
chairs, a front terrace under red and blue-striped awnings full of potted palms, a lawn with
string lights, and a big red-white-blue hot-air-balloon sign carrying its script logo (photos).
Families and groups of friends come to hang out (reviews 1, 4), and people like that they can
have coffee and a full meal in one place (review 4). Reviewers single out the beef teriyaki
(review 3), an open, calm, breezy rooftop that's beautiful at night (review 1), cleanliness,
good service and plenty of parking (review 2), and a nice atmosphere with friendly staff
(review 5). Google rating 4.4 from 546 reviews (listing). The sampled reviews are 3 to 5 years
old, so the features they mention need the owner's confirmation.

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Parent planning a weekend family outing | know if it suits kids and how late it's open on Saturday | see the weekend 23.00 closing and the family section without hunting |
| Neighbour from Billy Moon / Pondok Kelapa on the way to work | check it's open at 07.00 for breakfast | see today's hours and "open now" in the first screen |
| Group of friends looking for an evening spot | see the space, especially the rooftop at night | reach "Ruang di Bonjur" and the night panel quickly |
| Driver coming from elsewhere in Jakarta Timur | find the right street inside the housing complex | tap directions, and read "Komp. Billy Moon, Blok CF 1" prominently |
| Someone organising a family gathering | ask about a table | call in one tap (WhatsApp/booking only if the owner confirms) |

## Primary actions
1. Petunjuk Arah (opens the Google Maps listing)  2. Telepon 0858-9139-6021  3. Lihat menu (jump to "Menu andalan")
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone (and action 2 beside it).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: reservation forms, delivery-app links,
a full menu or menu PDF, English version, live music, any use of owner-3.jpg or customer photos.

## Open questions for the owner
(In Indonesian, for `owner-checklist.md`.)
1. Apakah Bonjur menyajikan sarapan mulai jam 07.00?
2. Apakah bubur ayam, nasi uduk, roti bakar dan kopi susu ada di menu sarapan?
3. Apakah nama menunya memang "Beef Teriyaki"?
4. Apakah Chicken Katsu, Ayam Geprek dan Nasi Goreng Bonjur ada di menu?
5. Apakah Pisang Goreng, Cireng dan Tahu Crispy ada di menu camilan?
6. Apakah Es Kopi Susu Gula Aren, Cappuccino, Teh Tarik dan Jus Buah Segar ada di menu minuman?
7. Apakah harga contoh ini mendekati harga sebenarnya: kopi mulai Rp 22rb, camilan mulai Rp 20rb, makanan berat mulai Rp 35rb?
8. Apakah area kelinci dan kolam ikan masih ada? (Kalau tidak, bagian "Seru untuk keluarga" kami sembunyikan.)
9. Apakah rooftop masih terbuka untuk tamu? (Ulasannya dari sekitar 3 tahun lalu.)
10. Apakah area parkir masih luas seperti di ulasan?
11. Apakah nomor 0858-9139-6021 bisa dihubungi lewat WhatsApp?
12. Apakah Bonjur menerima pesanan tempat untuk acara keluarga?
13. Apakah Anda setuju sapaan "Bonjur!" dipakai dengan gaya ceria, sebagai plesetan "bonjour"?
14. Apakah gaya bahasa santai dengan "kamu" cocok, atau Anda lebih suka "Anda"?
15. Apakah cocok kalau kami menyebut Bonjur sebagai tempat dari pagi sampai malam: sarapan, makan siang keluarga, dan malam akhir pekan?
16. Apakah cocok kalau halaman ini mengajak tetangga mampir sarapan sebelum berangkat kerja, dan keluarga dengan anak kecil?
