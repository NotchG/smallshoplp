## Personality
A family cafe-and-resto spread over a house plot in the Billy Moon housing complex: a glass-walled dining
room with a green wall, crystal chandeliers and walnut chairs, a front terrace under red and blue-striped
awnings crowded with potted palms, a lawn with string lights, and a big red-white-blue hot-air-balloon
sign carrying the logo (photos). Reviewers name an open, breezy, calm rooftop that's lovely at night
(review 1), plus a rabbit area and a fish pond (review 2).
- [MOCKUP] The name and red-white-blue balloon read as a playful French nod ("bonjour"). The site can use a cheerful greeting without pretending to be a French bistro.
- [MOCKUP] A place that runs all day for the neighbourhood: breakfast from 07:00, family lunches, and long weekend evenings.
