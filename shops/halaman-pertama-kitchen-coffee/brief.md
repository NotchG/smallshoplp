## Personality
A big, airy all-day cafe with a tropical garden feel: white rooms with tall arched windows, an open courtyard under a branch-and-thatch pergola, and a roofed terrace, so people stay from lunch into the night to work, eat dinner or hang out (reviews 1, 3, 4, 5; photos). It is as much a kitchen as a coffee shop; reviewers come for the food and the space more than for the coffee (reviews 1, 2, 3).
- The garden setting reminds visitors of a Bandung cafe, cool and green (review 3)
- [MOCKUP] The name works twice: "halaman" is both a page and a courtyard, so the page can play on "the first page of your day" and "the yard you settle into"
- [MOCKUP] An easy, unhurried place where nobody minds if you stay for hours with a laptop
