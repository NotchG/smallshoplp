# PRD: Halaman Pertama Kitchen & Coffee mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Halaman Pertama's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their cup
wordmark, their arched windows, their own food photo), and the checklist questions are few
enough to answer in one conversation.

## The shop in one paragraph
Halaman Pertama Kitchen & Coffee is a big, airy all-day cafe at Jl. Raya PKP No. 2, Kelapa Dua
Wetan, Ciracas, open every day from 08.30 to 22.30 (Fri–Sun to 23.00). It has three distinct
areas: an air-conditioned room that families with children use, an open courtyard that
sometimes has live performances, and a roofed outdoor terrace for smokers that keeps the sun
off (review 5). People come to work for hours (plenty of charging points, cold AC), to eat
dinner, and to hang out from late afternoon into the night. One reviewer says it feels like a
Bandung cafe (reviews 1–5). Reviewers come for the space and the kitchen more than for the
coffee: bulgogi rice, sushi maki, thin-crust pepperoni pizza, fries, and Bonbon Noir, one
regular's favourite drink. The menu has grown and service has got faster (review 2). Google
rating 4.1 from 596 reviews, price level moderate. Car parking is limited; motorbike parking
is available (reviews 2, 4).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out for this shop: Instagram link (no
handle in the listing), Wi-Fi claim, any customer photo (`photo-*.jpg`), a recreation of the
cup-and-leaf logo mark (we ask the owner for the file instead).
