# PRD: Waltie's Rooftop Café mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Waltie's Rooftop Café's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia (`lang="id"`) for all copy, buttons, hours and the draft
banner. Menu and dish names stay as the shop writes them (Soto Betawi, Bolvano).

## The shop in one paragraph
Waltie's Rooftop Café is a relaxed rooftop café at Arion Mall, Jl. Paus No.1, Rawamangun
(Pulo Gadung, Jakarta Timur), open every day 09.00–22.30, moderately priced, rated 4.6 from
432 Google reviews. It has an open-air terrace with tiled tables, wood-and-metal chairs, a
wall of potted plants and string lights, plus an air-conditioned indoor room with a "Free
Wifi Area" sign. Friends come to catch up, some people come alone to chill, office workers
come to unwind after work, and guests stay for hours. The menu runs from Indonesian comfort
food (the Soto Betawi gets a mention) to Western classics, with coffee, colourful bowls like
the Bolvano, ice blends and snack platters. Reviewers praise how cosy it is, the friendly
staff who give recommendations, the food, and a bill lower than they expected.
(brief.md: Basics, Personality, Audience, Signature products, What customers praise)
