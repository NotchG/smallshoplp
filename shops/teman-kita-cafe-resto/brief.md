## Personality
A friendly, slightly hidden neighbourhood cafe-resto in a residential street of Matraman, the kind of place locals know (reviews 2, 3, 4). It's a sketchbook-style space: white brick walls covered in black line drawings and hand-lettered coffee quotes, red metal chairs, and a big street-scene mural upstairs (photos). The name means "our friend", and that's the feeling: a relaxed spot for friends, family or a bit of me-time (reviews 1, 5).
- Two floors: indoor seating on the ground floor, a semi-outdoor area upstairs (reviews 2; photos)
- It serves coffee and drinks plus Indonesian food, from snacks to full meals, including some healthy options (reviews 1, 2, 3; type)
- People book the place for weddings and gatherings (reviews 2, 5)
- [MOCKUP] Teman Kita sees itself as the "ruang tamu" (living room) of the neighbourhood, a place to drop by any time
- [MOCKUP] The doodle walls and window lettering were done by hand for the shop
