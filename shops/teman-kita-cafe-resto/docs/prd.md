# PRD: Teman Kita Cafe & Resto mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Teman Kita Cafe & Resto's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout (copy, buttons, hours, draft banner, `lang="id"`).
Register: "kamu" [MOCKUP].

## The shop in one paragraph
Teman Kita is a cafe and Indonesian restaurant tucked into a residential street in Utan Kayu
Selatan, Matraman, the kind of place locals know (reviews 2, 3, 4). It has two floors: indoor
seating downstairs and a semi-outdoor upper floor with fans, a pastel street mural and red, mint
and gunmetal metal chairs (reviews 2; photos). The walls are white brick covered in black line
drawings and hand-lettered coffee quotes, and the name is painted on the wall in black brush
script (photos). It serves coffee and drinks plus Indonesian food, from snacks to full meals,
including healthy options (reviews 1, 2, 3; type). People mention the charcoal latte, thick
toast, fried tofu and a fried banana with milk and cheese that isn't too sweet (reviews 3, 4),
and the owner's own photo shows an orange juice float (owner-2). Friends, families, people after
some me-time and small meetups come here, and the space gets booked for weddings and reunions
(reviews 1, 2, 3, 5). Customers like that it's cosy, clean, nicely decorated, has lots of choice
and is affordable (reviews 1, 3, 4, 5). Google rating 4.5 from 785 reviews (listing). Open daily,
10.00–22.00, Friday from 12.30 (hours). The five listing reviews are 4–8 years old.

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Local from the surrounding streets | know if it's open today and drop by | see today's hours and the directions button in the first screen, on a phone |
| Friends planning a hangout or reunion | see the vibe and check it isn't booked out | see both floors; reach WhatsApp from the first screen and again at "Acara" |
| Couple or family planning a wedding or event | know the place hosts events and ask about it | find "Acara di Teman Kita" with a WhatsApp button prefilled for an availability question |
| Someone after me-time or a quiet coffee | judge if it's comfortable and what's on the menu | see the indoor floor and the drinks and snacks with example prices |
| First-timer coming from Google Maps | find a "hidden" place in a residential street | see the map, the full address and a directions link |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also kept off the page until the owner confirms:
halal claim, parking, AC or "cool and quiet" for either floor, Wi-Fi, the wafer, any review date.

## Open questions for the owner
Becomes `owner-checklist.md`. Each is yes/no; a "no" means "please send the right version".
1. Can people reach you on WhatsApp at 0878-8070-4666 for table and event questions?
2. Is "kamu" the right voice for the site? (Or would you prefer "Anda", at least for weddings and events?)
3. Are the coffee names and prices right: Kopi Susu Gula Aren Rp 22rb, Charcoal Latte Rp 28rb, Americano Rp 20rb, Cappuccino Rp 25rb?
4. Are the non-coffee drinks right: Es Jeruk Float Rp 25rb (the orange float in your photo), Es Teh Manis Rp 10rb, Matcha Latte Rp 27rb?
5. Are the snack names and prices right: Pisang Goreng Susu Keju Rp 20rb, Tahu Goreng Rp 18rb, Roti Bakar Tebal (cokelat-keju or srikaya) Rp 22rb?
6. Are the main dishes right: Nasi Goreng Teman Kita, Mie Goreng Jawa, Nasi Ayam Penyet, Indomie Telur Kornet, Rp 22rb–40rb?
7. Do you still serve healthy options like a grilled chicken rice bowl and a salad bowl, around Rp 35rb–45rb?
8. Are Kentang Goreng, Cireng and a Mix Platter (Rp 18rb–35rb) on the menu for sharing?
9. Do you offer event and wedding packages, priced per person or as a whole-space buy-out?
10. Are the charcoal latte, thick toast, fried tofu and fried banana with milk and cheese still on the menu? (The reviews mentioning them are 4–8 years old.)
11. Do the two floors still look like this: indoor downstairs, semi-outdoor upstairs with fans and the street mural?
12. Can you send photos of the ground floor, a coffee or charcoal latte, the fried banana and toast, and an event set-up, to replace the drawings?
13. Can you send your logo file, so the real logo replaces the brush-script lettering?
14. Is there parking you'd like us to mention?
15. Is the shop halal-certified, and can you share the certificate details to show on the site?
