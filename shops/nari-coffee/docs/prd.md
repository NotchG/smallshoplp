# PRD: Nari Coffee mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Nari Coffee's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Language: all copy, buttons, hours, alt text and the draft banner in Bahasa Indonesia, `lang="id"`.
Name on the page: "Nari Coffee", with "Duren Sawit" only as a location label (brief, name note).

## The shop in one paragraph
Nari Coffee is a clean, modern two-storey cafe on Jl. Swadaya Raya in Duren Sawit, Jakarta Timur,
with a cobalt-blue sign, a round white "N" badge, a black awning and a black steel staircase up to a
roofed upper level (photos). Inside: marble-look tables, glossy teak chairs, a grey marble bar under
a rounded LED ring light, no smoking indoors; outside, small filigree metal tables on a terrazzo-style
forecourt (photos). Food is as much the draw as the coffee: reviewers name Luna Coffee, and the window
poster announces Mie Blenger Original, Kari and Rendang (reviews 1; photos). Friends come to hang out,
couples come on dates, and people praise the cosy vibe, tasty food and drinks, friendly prices and very
nice service (reviews 1–5). Rated 4.9 from 315 Google reviews; open daily 10:00–21:30 per the listing
(listing, hours).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos beyond the two owner
photos (illustrations stand in for products), multiple pages, live open/closed status, any mention
of other branches, delivery apps or social accounts (none are in the brief).

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is "Nari Coffee" the right name, with "Duren Sawit" as the location label? Is this your only outlet (no other branches or central brand page we should link)?
2. The door sign says weekdays 10.00–22.00 and weekends 10.00–24.00, the Google listing says every day 10.00–21.30. Are the door-sign hours the correct ones?
3. Is the address "Jl. Swadaya Raya No.1" (the listing writes "No.1 2")?
4. Does 0895-1021-7613 take WhatsApp messages?
5. Is Luna Coffee your house signature iced coffee? (Tell us what goes in it and we'll write one line.)
6. Is the iced berry drink with cream called "Strawberry Latte"?
7. Is the rice bowl with glazed meat called "Rice Bowl Ayam Teriyaki"?
8. Are the fries listed as "French Fries"?
9. What is the noodle dish with meatballs and crispy wonton called? Is "Mie bakso & pangsit goreng" OK as a placeholder?
10. Is the drink called "Taro Latte"? Are Kopi Susu Aren, Americano, Cafe Latte, Matcha Latte and Lychee Tea on your menu?
11. Are these prices right: Mie Blenger Original 14rb, Kari 15rb, Rendang 15rb, Luna Coffee 25rb, French Fries 18rb, Rice Bowl 28rb?
12. Is Mie Blenger still your "new menu", or should the section just call it a favourite?
13. Is the upstairs level extra seating that suits groups?
14. Is the hero line "Kopi enak, makan kenyang, dompet tetap aman." OK?
15. Are the short section lines OK ("Datang buat kopinya, betah karena makanannya.", "Tiga rasa, tinggal pilih.", "Ada pertanyaan? Kabari kami lewat WhatsApp atau telepon.")?
16. Is the casual "kamu" tone OK, or do you prefer "Anda"?
17. Can we use your shopfront photo and the berry drink photo on the site? (The shopfront was taken while closed; we cropped out the motorbikes.)
18. Can you send your logo file? The round N badge on the mockup is cropped from the sign photo.
19. The drinks and Mie Blenger are drawn illustrations. Would you rather send photos of Luna Coffee, the taro drink and the three Mie Blenger?
20. Is it OK to show "4,9 di Google Maps dari 315 ulasan" on the page?
21. Is smoking allowed on the front forecourt? (The page only says indoors is no-smoking; we can add this if you want.)
