# PRD: Clay Coffee Space mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Clay Coffee Space's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"` are in Bahasa Indonesia (brief,
Tone of voice). Register "kamu" [MOCKUP].

## The shop in one paragraph
Clay Coffee Space is a coffee shop and cafe on Jl. Jatinegara Kaum No.17, Pulo Gadung, Jakarta
Timur, listed as open 24 hours every day, moderately priced, rated 4.3 from 743 Google reviews
(listing). It is a big open-air coffee garden that comes alive after dark: a gravel courtyard,
tan market umbrellas, festoon string lights and LED strips along the paths, all around a
white-framed glass pavilion that holds a "Podcast Box", framed by a graffiti mural and a
wood-plank wall (photos). Drinks come in clear branded "CLAY" cups and snacks in kraft paper
boxes (photos). People come in groups; reviewers praise the vibe and ambience for groups, the
coffee, and the live music on a stage on Saturday nights, and one finds it relatively
affordable (reviews 1, 3, 5). Food gets mixed comments and one review describes slow service,
so the page leads with coffee, the space and the music, and promises nothing about speed
(reviews 1, 2, 3).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Customer photos (`photo-*.jpg`) never appear.
No reviewer names, no quoted reviews, no claims of fast service, no "terbaik"-style superlatives.

## Open questions for the owner
1. The listing says Clay is open 24 hours, every day. Is that right, so the page can say "Buka 24 jam"?
2. Does 0877-9140-1139 take WhatsApp messages for questions and bookings?
3. Can we call the house iced coffee "Es Kopi Susu Clay" and say it's made with palm sugar, at Rp 22rb?
4. Are these coffee drinks and prices close: Americano Rp 20rb, Cafe Latte Rp 25rb, Cappuccino Rp 25rb, Caramel Latte and Hazelnut Latte Rp 28rb?
5. Are Matcha Latte, Taro Latte, Vanilla Milk and Lychee Tea on the menu at about Rp 22rb–28rb?
6. Is the fried snack with chocolate and cheese called "Pisang Goreng Coklat Keju", at about Rp 20rb?
7. Is the mix plate called "Mix Platter" (kentang goreng, sosis, chicken pop), at about Rp 35rb?
8. Are Cireng Rujak, Tahu Cabe Garam and Kentang Goreng on the menu at about Rp 18rb–25rb?
9. For late nights, are Rice Bowl Ayam Sambal Matah, Nasi Goreng Clay and Indomie Goreng Telur on the menu at about Rp 20rb–38rb?
10. Does Saturday live music start around 20.00?
11. Can the Podcast Box be booked by the hour, from Rp 75rb/jam, through WhatsApp, for recording or online meetings?
12. Is it fair to describe Clay as a late-night ngopi spot for after work, after futsal or after a gig?
13. Are night owls, students and shift workers from around Pulo Gadung and Jatinegara a big part of your crowd?
14. Do content creators and small teams use the Podcast Box?
15. Should the page say "kamu" to visitors (rather than "Anda")?
16. Are you happy with the page's short intro lines (hero line, section intros, closing line), written for this mockup?
17. Does the name "Clay" stand for something handmade and down-to-earth? (Not used on the page yet; only if you'd like it told.)
18. Can you send photos of the courtyard at night, the glass pavilion, the Podcast Box, the stage on a Saturday night, and a few drinks in the branded cups, plus your logo file? They replace the drawn placeholders.
