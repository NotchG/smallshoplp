## Personality
A bright, homey family cafe that is just as much a kitchen: a long menu of Indonesian and Western plates, crafted drinks, and a proper indoor playroom, so parents eat well while the kids play (reviews 1, 3, 4; photos). The staff are warm and will tune a drink to your taste (reviews 1, 2, 4).
- Clean modern rooms in white, teak and cognac leather, livened up by bold colourful paintings (photos)
- The owner's own photos are polished studio shots, which points to a shop that takes its food and drinks presentation seriously (photos)
- [MOCKUP] "Madre" is Spanish for "mother", which suits a family-first cafe; the owner can tell the real story behind the name

## Signature products
- Ayam Afrika: roast chicken leg on spiced nasi kebuli rice with fried plantain and three sauces (green, white, red sambal); a long-time favourite of one regular (reviews 1, 5; photos)
- Beef Dabu-Dabu, smoky beef served with mashed potato (review 5)
- Gyudon Andaliman, a beef rice bowl with andaliman pepper (review 1)
- Cordon Bleu with mashed potato, salad and a mushroom cream sauce (review 5; photos)
- Black Basil, a signature espresso drink with strawberry syrup and grape juice; the bar will add espresso if you like it stronger (review 1)
