# PRD: ULUSALU coffee and roastery mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes ULUSALU coffee and roastery's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their bar wall of
black slats and warm lights, their nasi goreng beside the black ULUSALU cup, their name), and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All copy, buttons, hours and the draft banner are in Bahasa Indonesia, with `lang="id"` (brief).
Menu names stay exactly as the shop uses them (brief).

## The shop in one paragraph
ULUSALU is a bright coffee shop and roastery at Jl. Jakarta Garden City Boulevard Unit A1,
Cakung Timur, in the JGC food garden area, and it also cooks proper meals (listing, reviews 1, 2).
Its drum roaster stands by floor-to-ceiling windows looking onto greenery. The room has black
vertical slats with warm stepped lighting over the bar, white brick, a glass pastry case, and
white tables with rattan-backed wooden chairs (photos). Families come for the main courses and
the pastries (Choco Banana and Tiramisu, plus Risoles and Sosis Solo for the children). Laptop
workers come because it is cozy and calm, with sockets at several tables (reviews 1, 2, 5).
People single out the Aren Latte, called the best in the JGC area, and the Mushroom Cream Soup
(review 4). It is open every day from 07:00: until 22:00 Sunday to Thursday, and until midnight
on Friday and Saturday (hours). It is rated 4.7 on Google from 91 reviews (listing).

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| JGC resident heading out (from Maps or Instagram) | know if it's open now and how to get there | see today's hours and "Petunjuk Arah" in the first screen on a phone |
| Parent planning a family meal | know there's real food and something the children like | see the favourites (Risoles, Sosis Solo, main courses) and the family/WFC section |
| Remote worker (WFC) | know it's calm, has sockets, and serves food and coffee all day | read "Colokan di beberapa meja" and the opening hours without hunting |
| Coffee drinker | find the Aren Latte and, if offered, beans to take home | see the Aren Latte among the favourites and the roaster section |
| The owner (the pitch) | recognise the shop and spot what to correct | see their photo, the slat wall, their dishes, and clearly marked mockup items |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Specific to this shop: no customer photos
(photo-1/2/3.jpg) anywhere on the site, even though two came from an account named like the shop;
no logo reproduction (the cup's script mark is shown only inside the owner photo); no bean shop
or WhatsApp ordering flow beyond a plain link.

## Open questions for the owner
Becomes `owner-checklist.md`.
1. Should the site use the name on your Google listing, "ULUSALU coffee and roastery", rather than "ULUSALU Coffee Roastery & Resto"?
2. Do you roast the coffee you serve on your own roaster in the shop?
3. Do you sell your own beans to take home, roughly like "ULUSALU House Blend, 200 g, mulai Rp 85rb"?
4. Do you offer manual brew with your own roasts, for example Gayo or Toraja?
5. Are these example prices close: Aren Latte Rp 28rb, Mushroom Cream Soup Rp 32rb, Nasi Goreng Rp 40rb? (If not, can you send the current menu with prices?)
6. Is "Kopi hitam dingin" right for your iced black coffee, or does the menu call it something else?
7. Is Twinings tea still on the menu?
8. Is the Spaghetti Aglio Olio still made with chopped chilli, so it should be marked "Pedas"?
9. Is the hero line "Kopi dari roaster kami, makan siang dari dapur kami." OK for you?
10. Is the casual "kamu" register right for your guests?
11. Is it fair to say guests come for breakfast when you open at 07:00?
12. Does 0812-8058-2016 take WhatsApp messages, including bean orders?
13. Is "Telepon dulu kalau mau tanya menu hari ini." a fair invitation to call?
14. Is the Google account "ULUSALU COFFEE ROASTERY & RESTO" yours? If yes, may we use its interior and mushroom soup photos on the site?
15. Are the drawn illustrations of your dishes OK until you send photos of them, and can you send your logo file?
