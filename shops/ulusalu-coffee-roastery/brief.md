## Personality
A bright coffee roastery that also cooks proper meals, loved by families and laptop workers
alike (reviews 2, 5). A drum coffee roaster stands by floor-to-ceiling windows looking onto
greenery; black vertical slats and warm stepped lighting over the bar, white brick, a glass
pastry case, white tables and rattan-backed wooden chairs (photos). Quiet, with no music
playing when one reviewer visited (review 1).
- [MOCKUP] Roasts its own beans, so the espresso and the beans to take home come from the same roaster. The roaster is in the photos and "roastery" is in the name, but the listing doesn't say what they roast.
