# PRD: Rumah Roti Ciracas mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Rumah Roti Ciracas's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their butter-yellow
walls, their turtle buns, their own sticker with the WhatsApp number), and the checklist
questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All site copy is in Bahasa Indonesia (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours and the draft banner.

## The shop in one paragraph
Rumah Roti Ciracas is a small, cheerful neighbourhood bakery in a two-storey shophouse at
Jl. Masjid Al-Ishlah No. 20, Susukan, Ciracas, Jakarta Timur (listing). It is open Monday to
Saturday 08.10–19.10 and closed on Sunday (hours), and is priced as inexpensive (listing). Alongside
everyday breads (garlic cheese buns, pizza bread by the tray, muffins, tall square loaves, a
cream-filled crumble bun) it bakes playful shaped breads (turtle buns with green-piped shells,
small crocodile breads with chocolate sprinkles), roti buaya pairs wrapped in cellophane with pink
ribbon for Betawi weddings and engagements, and birthday cakes decorated to order (photos). Its
logo sticker, with the WhatsApp number 0838-9818-8891, goes on its cakes and bread bags (photos).
Customers praise good bread with a texture they love, great taste, friendly service and very good
value, and say it looks as good as food on Instagram (reviews 1–5). Rated 4.9 from 736 Google
reviews (listing).

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Neighbour heading home on a motorbike | know if the shop is open today | see today's hours in the first screen on a phone; get directions in one tap |
| Family preparing a lamaran or Betawi wedding | order roti buaya | see the wrapped pairs, the lead time and starting price [MOCKUP], and open a WhatsApp chat |
| Parent planning a birthday | see what the cakes look like and how to order | see two of the shop's cakes, the starting price [MOCKUP], and a WhatsApp button |
| Someone who saw the turtle buns or reviews online | see what is on the shelf and what it costs | see at least 3 everyday products with names and example prices [MOCKUP] |
| First-time visitor from Google Maps | trust the place and find it | see 4.9 from 736 reviews, paraphrased praise, the address, a map and the hours table |

## Primary actions
1. WhatsApp (ask what's available, or order; wa.me/6283898188891)  2. Petunjuk Arah (Google Maps directions)  3. Telepon (0838-9818-8891)
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone (the WhatsApp button and the shop's
sticker both link to WhatsApp; Petunjuk Arah sits beside the WhatsApp button).

The brief's draft "Roti bentuk lucu" section is folded into the hero (turtle buns) and Roti buaya
(small crocodile breads), so no owner photo is used twice.

Copy register is "kamu"/neutral [MOCKUP]; most lines avoid pronouns.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out: any customer photo (photo-1, photo-2),
review quotes or reviewer names, delivery, car parking, a reproduction of the shop's logo artwork.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Do you take cake and roti buaya pre-orders over WhatsApp?
2. Do you bake fresh every day before opening (the hero says "dipanggang setiap hari")?
3. Are you a go-to for neighbourhood birthdays and Betawi weddings? (Not claimed on the page; ask before we add it.)
4. Do parents buy the fun-shaped buns for kids' snacks and school events? (Not claimed on the page; ask before we add it.)
5. Are these the names you use: Roti Garlic Cheese, Roti Kura-Kura, Roti Buaya (sepasang), Roti Pizza, Roti Tawar, Muffin, Roti Krim?
6. Are these prices about right: everyday sweet breads Rp 3rb–8rb, Roti Garlic Cheese Rp 10rb, Roti Pizza Rp 45rb per loyang?
7. Do roti buaya pairs start at Rp 150rb, and birthday cakes at Rp 150rb?
8. Is 2–3 days' notice right for roti buaya and cake orders?
9. Should we leave Muffin & Roti Tawar without a price, or do you want to add one?
10. Is "kamu" the right tone, or would you prefer "Anda"?
11. Are your hours exactly 08.10–19.10, Monday to Saturday, closed on Sunday?
12. Which shopfront is current: the cream sign with the green-orange-white awning (your photo), or a white sign with a teal awning? Is it fine to show your shopfront photo small?
13. Is it fine to show the chocolate birthday cake photo cropped so the child's name doesn't show?
14. Is the sticker on the site close enough to yours? Can you send the original logo file so we can use the real one?
15. Is the map pin on the right building?
16. Are the example section lines (the hero line, "Chat kami aja", the prefilled WhatsApp messages) fine as written?
