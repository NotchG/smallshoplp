## Personality
A roomy neighbourhood coffee house in an old white clapboard house with a turf garden, cosy rattan-and-concrete rooms and a rooftop with tiered concrete seating, built for hanging out with friends (photos, reviews 1, 3, 4). Relaxed, good music, friendly baristas, prices that don't scare anyone off (reviews 4, 5).
- Many spaces, many moods: garden under string lights, a reading nook with bookshelves and neon signs, a pebble-floored corner with rattan chairs, a rooftop amphitheatre (photos)
- Family-friendly touches: high chairs for babies indoors (photos)
- A photobox booth inside (photos)
- People make memories here and come back for years (review 2)
- A cat wanders the garden (photos)
- [MOCKUP] Open from early morning, so it works for a first coffee before work as well as a late night on the rooftop (hours).
