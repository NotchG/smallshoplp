# Publika Eat and Coffee

## Basics
- Type: Cafe, also a restaurant (type)
- Address: Jalan Jakarta Garden City Blok B2 No. 11, Cakung Timur, Cakung, Jakarta Timur 13910 (listing)
- Phone: not listed (listing)
- Hours: Mon–Fri 09:00–21:30 · Sat–Sun 07:00–21:30 (hours)
- Price level: Moderate (listing)
- Google rating: 4.5 from 1,467 reviews (listing)

## Personality
A big, airy all-day cafe-restaurant in JGC with a wide menu of Indonesian food, coffee and tea,
where groups of any size get tables pushed together and solo workers settle in with the wifi
(reviews 1, 2, 3, 5). High ceilings with exposed dark steel and timber beams, hanging ferns,
copper and globe pendants, tan leather banquettes and rattan chairs (photos).
- [MOCKUP] The dependable "let's meet at Publika" spot for JGC: breakfast, a work session, a family dinner.

## Audience
- Friends and families, from small groups to large ones (review 1)
- People working remotely: stable wifi and quick service (review 2)
- Casual lunches, dinners and group meetings (review 3)
- Visitors with appointments in the Cakung area (review 1)
- [MOCKUP] Early weekend crowd: it opens at 07:00 on Saturdays and Sundays (hours), so weekend breakfast is likely a draw.

## What customers praise
- Spacious, with high ceilings and room for groups (reviews 1, 3)
- Staff rearrange tables to fit your group (review 1)
- Stable wifi and quick service, good for working (review 2)
- Wide menu, good taste, nice plating (reviews 1, 3, 5)
- Fair prices for the portion size (review 4)
- Notes: the air conditioning isn't very cool (review 1), and it can get noisy, so bring headphones to focus (review 2). Reviews 1, 3 and 5 are 3–4 years old, so the menu may have changed.
