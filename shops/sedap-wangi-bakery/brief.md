## Personality
A long-running bakery and diner in one: cakes, breads and pastries in glass cases at the front, and a kitchen serving noodles, soto and Indonesian dishes (reviews 1, 3, 5; photos). Reviewers call it legendary, a bakery they've known since childhood (reviews 3, 4).
- Old-school ("jadul") cakes with a taste of their own (review 4)
- Bread made in-house, fresh from the oven (review 3)
- Familiar and generous, a place families keep coming back to (reviews 3, 4)
- Runs deals: discounts and bread bundles (review 1)
- [MOCKUP] A neighbourhood institution where people stop for a bowl of sotomie and leave with a box of cake
