## Personality
A small, snug, quiet coffee house tucked away from the bustle of Buaran, with a rustic-chic room of wood, leather and dark walls, where people come for proper coffee, a calm chat or a few hours of work (description; reviews 1, 2, 3, 4).
- Neighbourhood cafe known for creative coffee drinks and light food (description)
- Serious about coffee: a manual-brew menu, cold drip towers on the counter, jars of beans by origin, and a reviewer who says they roast on site where you can watch (review 5; photos)
- A quiet place without loud live music, which is part of the appeal (review 1)
- [MOCKUP] The black-and-white checked awning, the carved figure inside and the "Made" name hint at a Balinese touch; the owner can confirm or tell the real story behind the name (photos)
