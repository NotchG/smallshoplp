# PRD: LINDAYES Cake & Bakery mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes LINDAYES Cake & Bakery's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their charcoal sign,
orange LINDAYES wordmark and orange awning are the first thing on screen), and the checklist
questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Site language: Bahasa Indonesia for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"`.
Product names stay as the shop uses them (LINDAYES, patisserie & coffee, Cake & Bakery, brownies, bagelen).

## The shop in one paragraph
A bright, contemporary bakeshop on Jl. Raya Penggilingan No. 14 in Penggilingan, Cakung, selling
cakes, brownies, cookies and breads (description). It has since become a bakery cafe (review 1),
which is why the sign reads "LINDAYES patisserie & coffee" (photos). The cafe corner has deep
teal walls, trailing ivy, a bougainvillea-and-Eiffel-Tower mural, bar stools and a glass cake
chiller of single-serve cakes, cupcakes, chocolate dome cakes and brownie bites (photos). Another
wall of lit wooden shelves holds packaged snacks such as bagelen, gift boxes and hamper baskets,
and at Lebaran the counter fills with shrink-wrapped towers of kue kering jars (photos). It is
listed as a bakery, pastry, cake and dessert shop with catering and food delivery (type), opens
every day 06:00–21:00 (hours), and holds 4.1 from 490 Google reviews (listing). Reviewers praise
the cakes and bread (reviews 3, 5), the brownies (review 3), good service (review 2) and a good,
pleasant bakery (review 1).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them; here only owner-1.jpg), multiple pages, a link to
yescakeandbakery.com, any customer photo (photo-1/2/3 are reference only).

## Open questions for the owner
1. Should the page use "LINDAYES Cake & Bakery" as the name, with "patisserie & coffee" as the tagline?
2. Should the page mention "Yes Cake & Bakery" (the rooftop sign and cake boxes)? If so, is it an earlier name or a sister brand?
3. yescakeandbakery.com is live. Is that your site, and should this page link to it (or replace it)?
4. Does the shopfront still look like your Google photo (charcoal sign, orange LINDAYES lettering, orange awning)?
5. Is the hero line "Sedikit suasana Paris di Jl. Raya Penggilingan…" right for you?
6. Is the gifts line "Andalan untuk hadiah: toples Lebaran, hampers dan kue dalam kotak…" right for you?
7. Are nastar, kastengel, putri salju and lidah kucing the kue kering you sell in jars?
8. Are roti sobek, roti tawar, roti abon and roti keju among your everyday breads?
9. Do you serve kopi susu gula aren, americano, cafe latte and es teh?
10. Are these example prices close: roti from Rp 8rb, slice cake Rp 25rb, brownies Rp 65rb per box, kue kering Rp 85rb per jar, coffee from Rp 18rb?
11. Do you take seasonal pre-orders for Lebaran, Natal and Imlek?
12. Do you take orders for snack boxes, birthday cakes and office orders?
13. Does 0818-0898-9381 take WhatsApp orders?
14. Is the Tokopedia shop (yescakeandbakery) still active, and should the page link to it?
15. Is the courteous "Anda" right, or would you prefer the more casual "kamu"?
16. Is it right to speak to early commuters ("roti untuk bekal pagi") and to offices and arisan groups ("snack box untuk kantor dan arisan")?
