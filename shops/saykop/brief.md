## Personality
A small, creative neighbourhood coffee shop with a bright orange mural wall, known for inventive signature drinks with their own names, and for being a calm place where people feel at ease working for hours (reviews 1, 4; photos). Staff are friendly and ask for feedback; in the evening they go table to table to check how the food and service were (reviews 1, 3).
- Drinks are more creative than at the other coffee shops nearby (review 4)
- [MOCKUP] Each signature drink has a short personal name (Axelle, Mariani, Elma, Misty) because the team names its creations, like characters on a menu
