## Tone of voice
Warm, patient and honest. Speaks to people who may be worried and embarrassed about debt:
calm, no judgement, simple words, practical next steps.
- Site language: Bahasa Indonesia, for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"` (project rule for Indonesian shops)
- Polite, everyday Indonesian addressing the reader as "Anda". A light religious register fits the firm (one review opens with "Alhamdulillah", review 3), but don't overdo it.
- Keep within the Indonesian advocates' code of ethics: no "terbaik", "pasti menang", "100% berhasil", "utang pasti lunas" or similar, no case results, no client names. A lawyer may not guarantee a client will win (Kode Etik Advokat Indonesia, Pasal 4c), and advertising purely to attract attention is restricted (Pasal 8).
- Never promise that debts will be cancelled. The firm can help, not erase.
