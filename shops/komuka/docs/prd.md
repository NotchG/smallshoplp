# PRD: KOMUKA Cafe & Eatery mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes KOMUKA's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

Display name on the page: **KOMUKA Cafe & Eatery** (the listing name is keyword-stuffed; brief).
Site language: Bahasa Indonesia throughout, `lang="id"`, including the draft banner, buttons,
alt text, aria labels, day names (Senin…Minggu) and times with dots (11.00–22.00).

## The shop in one paragraph
KOMUKA is a warm, cozy cafe and eatery on Jl. Menteng Timur in Ujung Menteng, Cakung, that
serves far more than coffee: tea, full meals and a long list of house signatures, from
Spaghetti Bolognese Brûlée and sop buntut to dirty chai, V60 and pudding mangga (reviews 1, 3, 4).
Families and groups of friends come for the many seating options and the leafy front terrace of
teak picnic tables under trees and string lights; others come for a quiet corner to work or read,
and groups can book a VIP room (reviews 1, 2, 5; photos). What people love most is the
hard-to-name comfort of the place and staff who happily help first-timers choose
(reviews 1, 2, 3). Open Wednesday to Sunday, closed Monday and Tuesday (hours); 4.9 from 2,441
Google reviews (listing).

| Organiser of a family or friends' gathering | book a private space | find "Ruang VIP" and tap "Pesan ruang VIP" (WhatsApp) |
| Remote worker or reader | a quiet corner and the hours | read "Di dalam" in the spaces section and the full hours table |
| Anyone on a Monday or Tuesday | avoid a wasted trip | see "TUTUP" on the sign and "Senin–Selasa tutup" made obvious in the hours |

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos beyond the three
owner uploads (other visuals are illustrations or placeholders), multiple pages, an English
version, the KOMUKA logo file (the name is set in the site's display face, not redrawn).

## Open questions for the owner
Phrased for `owner-checklist.md` (Bahasa Indonesia, "Anda" register for the owner):
1. Harga contoh di halaman (kopi mulai Rp 28rb, camilan mulai Rp 25rb, makanan berat mulai Rp 45rb): apakah sudah mendekati harga KOMUKA?
2. Apakah V60 KOMUKA memakai biji single origin Indonesia yang bergantian, seperti Gayo, Kintamani, dan Toraja?
3. Apakah ada menu sarapan khusus hari Minggu mulai 09.00, misalnya bubur ayam dan paket roti?
4. Apakah nomor 0811-9117-845 bisa dihubungi lewat WhatsApp?
5. Apakah ruang VIP bisa dipesan lewat WhatsApp di nomor yang sama?
6. Boleh kami menyebut KOMUKA "ruang tamunya warga Metland Menteng dan JGC" dan mengajak keluarga datang hari Minggu?
7. Apakah gaya bahasa santai dengan "kamu" sudah cocok (bukan "Anda")?
8. Boleh nama-nama menu yang unik (Spaghetti Bolognese Brûlée, Soul Sister) ditonjolkan seperti di halaman ini?
9. Untuk nasi goreng di foto Anda (daging berlapis wijen, telur mata sapi, kerupuk, acar), boleh kami pakai label sementara sampai Anda kirim nama menunya?
10. Apakah ejaan dan kategori menu sudah benar (Komuka chips, Lumpia, Kwetiau goreng, Sop buntut, Dirty chai, V60, Pudding mangga, Signature Toast, Spaghetti Bolognese Brûlée, Soul Sister di "Kopi & teh", Hojicha latte, Caramel coffee)?
11. Gambar sop buntut, lumpia, pudding mangga dan dirty chai adalah ilustrasi contoh. Bersediakah Anda mengirim foto asli untuk menggantinya?
12. Boleh kami memakai tiga foto yang Anda unggah ke Google (teras depan, nasi goreng, tampak depan) di situs ini?
13. Halaman memakai tulisan KOMUKA biasa, bukan logo Anda. Bersediakah Anda mengirim file logonya?
