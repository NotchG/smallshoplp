A small, unassuming shophouse coffee bar in Condet that surprises people. It looks plain from the street, but
inside it's serious about coffee, with manual brew, a full brewing kit and hours that run past midnight
(reviews 2, 4, hours, photos).
- It looks like an ordinary small coffee shop from outside, but offers plenty of coffee options, manual brew included (reviews 2)
