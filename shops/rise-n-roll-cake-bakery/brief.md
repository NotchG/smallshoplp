## Personality
A small, bright cafe-bakery in Lubang Buaya that people come back to again and again. One regular says they've been about ten times. They come for the cheesecake and stay for comfort food (reviews 1, 2, 3).
It's clean and comfortable, and the staff greet you warmly (reviews 3, 5). Inside: a sage-green ceiling, white walls, terrazzo floor, dark wood tables, mustard chairs, fresh pink roses on every table, paper lanterns, and orange arched shelving behind a glass cake counter (photos).
There's also an outdoor area (review 1) and a prayer room (review 3).
