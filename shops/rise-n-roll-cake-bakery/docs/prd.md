# PRD: Rise n Roll Cake & Bakery mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Rise n Roll Cake & Bakery's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All site copy is in Bahasa Indonesia (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours, alt text and the
draft banner. Dish names stay exactly as the shop uses them (Biscoff cheesecake, mac n cheese,
matah chicken bowl, bitterballen, carrot cake, sus krakelin chocolate, pie susu).

## The shop in one paragraph
A small, bright cafe-bakery on Jl. Monumen Pancasila Sakti in Lubang Buaya, Jakarta Timur,
rated 4.9 from 290 Google reviews. Under a sage-green ceiling, with white walls, a terrazzo floor,
dark wood tables, mustard chairs, a pink rose on every table and orange arched shelving behind
a glass cake counter (photos). People come for the cheesecake, called creamy and rich without
being heavy, with blueberry and Biscoff named by reviewers (reviews 1, 2). Many stay for
the cafe food: risol ayam keju, mac n cheese, matah chicken bowl, bitterballen, carrot cake,
sus krakelin chocolate, pie susu, and a latte (reviews 1, 3). Regulars praise the clean, comfortable
room, warm staff and fair prices; there is an outdoor area and a prayer room, parking is small,
and Sunday afternoons fill up (reviews 1, 3, 4, 5). Open every day, 08.00–21.00 on weekdays and
08.00–22.00 at weekends (hours).

3. Pre-order a whole cheesecake via WhatsApp [MOCKUP placeholder; no number in the listing]

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also out: wholesale/bulk ordering (only hinted
by listing types), an "open now / closed" badge (the hero states today's hours instead), star widgets.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Should the site use the name "Rise n Roll Cake & Bakery" (rather than "Rise n Roll Cake, Pastry, and More..")?
2. Are the hours right: Senin–Jumat 08.00–21.00, Sabtu–Minggu 08.00–22.00? (Your door decal says 07.00–21.00.)
3. Is the hero line right: "Cheesecake yang creamy dan kaya rasa, tapi tetap ringan. Mampir untuk sepotong, betah sampai makan siang."?
4. Would you like the full English tagline from your window ("…sweeter … our cakes") as the hero line instead? If yes, what is the full text?
5. Is it fair to describe Rise n Roll as a cheesecake shop first and a cafe second?
6. Do you bake everything in-house without preservatives, and may the site say so? If yes, what exact wording is on your door?
7. Do you take whole-cheesecake pre-orders for birthdays and gatherings?
8. Are the whole-cake sizes 16 cm and 20 cm?
9. Do you make Original, Matcha and Klepon cheesecake? If not, which other flavours do you have?
10. Are the short flavour notes right (Blueberry: "Blueberry yang kaya rasa di atas keju yang lembut"; Biscoff: "Dengan sentuhan kayu manis")?
11. Are kopi susu gula aren, matcha latte and es teh on your drinks menu?
12. Are these example prices close: cheesecake Rp 35rb per slice, whole 16 cm from Rp 250rb, coffee from Rp 25rb, risol Rp 15rb, mains from Rp 40rb?
13. Should the site speak to guests as "kamu" (casual) rather than "Anda"?
14. Is the section copy right ("Cheesecake kami", "Bukan cuma kue", "Ruang kami" and the "Hubungi kami" line)?
15. Do you have high chairs for guests' children, as the site says?
16. Can you share your WhatsApp number for orders, so the WhatsApp button works?
17. Can you share your Instagram handle, so the Instagram button works?
18. May we use your own photos (the dining room, the choux puffs with chocolate sauce)? If yes, please re-share them from the business account so they're marked as yours. What is the choux dessert called on your menu?
