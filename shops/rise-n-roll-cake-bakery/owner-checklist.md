# Owner checklist: Rise n Roll Cake & Bakery

Everything on the mockup that needs the owner's yes, no or correction. Items tagged [MOCKUP]
carry `data-mockup` on the page; all copy and facts live in `site/content.ts`, so each answer
is a one-line edit. Facts taken from the Google listing (address, rating, reviews) are not
listed here unless the owner might want them worded differently.
