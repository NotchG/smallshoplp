# PRD: kupinami Cafe & Resto mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes kupinami's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

The five-second test here: the page is their mint mural wall with their teal cup and
their name, it says "tanpa musik" up front, and it reserves through the same WhatsApp
number as their own hours graphic.

## The shop in one paragraph
kupinami (listed as "Kupinami Cafè & Resto"; the shop's own logo and handles say "kupinami")
is a calm family cafe-restaurant at Jl. Bina Marga No. 44, Ceger, Cipayung, Jakarta Timur
(listing). Google files it as a noodle shop, but it is a family cafe-resto with a proper
coffee bar and a mixed Indonesian and Western menu: Mie Aceh beef wagyu and mie goreng
oriental next to chicken carbonara, fish and chips and pastries (reviews 1, 3, 4, 5; photos).
There is deliberately no music, a big children's playground, a clean prayer room, a
spacious car park and an upstairs floor for events (reviews 1, 2, 4). The coffee is taken
seriously: careful espresso drinks, and a cappuccino comes with a peanut cookie and two
kinds of sugar so you choose the sweetness (reviews 3, 4). Open every day 07:00–22:00
(hours), with a Friday break for Jum'atan 11:00–13:00 on the shop's own graphic (photos:
owner-2). Rated 4.7 from 2,883 Google reviews (listing). Moderate prices (listing).

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: delivery ordering flows, chat widgets,
a floating WhatsApp button, the owner-2 hours graphic as a page image, any customer photo.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is the Friday break for Jum'atan (11.00–13.00) still in place, and should the site show it?
2. Are these example prices close enough to show: kopi mulai Rp 25rb, camilan mulai Rp 25rb, Mie Aceh mulai Rp 40rb, pasta mulai Rp 50rb, Mie Aceh beef wagyu Rp 75rb?
3. Do you serve Mie Aceh goreng, tumis and kuah, with daging, udang or seafood?
4. Do you have a kids' menu, for example chicken nuggets with fries and a small spaghetti bolognese?
5. Do you serve Kopi Gayo on manual brew? Do you roast your own beans (Google lists you as a coffee roastery)?
6. Can the upstairs floor be booked for arisan, kids' birthdays, family gatherings and office meet-ups?
7. Are "fish and chips" and the omelette with beef from your photo called that on your menu? If not, what are their names?
8. Does the page's wording sound like you ("Tanpa musik. Cukup suara obrolan.", "Tempat makan keluarga yang tenang di Cipayung", a weekend-lunch place for the whole family)?
9. Is addressing guests as "Anda" right, or would you prefer "kamu"?
10. We redrew your logo from your hours graphic. Can you send the original file, and should the name be written "Cafe" (no accent)?
11. Are instagram.com/kupinami and facebook.com/kupinami your pages?
12. Would you like the story behind the name "kupinami" on the page? If so, what does it mean?
13. Do you hold a halal certificate you'd like shown? (We haven't shown one.)
