# Kupinami Cafe & Resto

The listing spells it "Kupinami Cafè & Resto", with a grave accent (listing). The shop's own logo
and social handles just say "kupinami" in lowercase (photos: owner-2). Use the "kupinami" wordmark
with "Cafe & Resto" as the descriptor, and drop the stray accent unless the owner wants it.

**What it really is:** Google's primary type is "Noodle Shop" (type), but it isn't a noodle shop.
Reviews describe a family cafe-restaurant: a proper coffee bar, a mixed Indonesian and Western menu
(Mie Aceh and mie goreng next to chicken carbonara and pastries), a children's playground, a prayer
room and no music (reviews 1–5). Noodles are one strong part of the menu, not the whole of it.
Google's own summary calls it a minimalist coffee shop with a kids' play area serving drinks and
light fare (description), but reviewers order full meals (reviews 1, 4, 5).

## Personality
A calm, family-first cafe-resto. There's deliberately no music, so it's quiet enough to talk, eat
or work, with a big children's playground, a clean prayer room (a mosque, per one reviewer), a
spacious car park and an upstairs floor for events (reviews 1, 2, 4). The coffee is taken
seriously: careful espresso drinks from knowledgeable baristas (review 3), and a cappuccino comes
with a peanut cookie and two kinds of sugar so you can choose the sweetness (review 4). Inside:
a mint-green wall painted with tropical flowers and leaves, wood-look floors, mint metal chairs and
teal cups (photos). Listed as a halal restaurant (type).
- [MOCKUP] A weekend-lunch place for the whole family: parents eat in peace while the kids play, and nobody has to shout over a speaker.
- [MOCKUP] Ask the owner what the name means. With Mie Aceh on the menu it may nod to Aceh, where coffee is "kupi". Don't put this on the page unless the owner confirms it.
