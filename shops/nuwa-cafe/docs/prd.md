# PRD: NUWA Cafe mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes NUWA Cafe's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds, and the
checklist questions are few enough to answer in one conversation.

All copy, buttons, hours and the draft banner are in Bahasa Indonesia, `lang="id"`. Name on the page: "NUWA Cafe".

## The shop in one paragraph
NUWA Cafe is a bright, white neighbourhood coffee shop on Jl. Gongseng Raya in Pasar Rebo, open every day
11.00–22.00, that works as much as a lunch place as a coffee stop. Outside it has a white fascia with "NUWA" in
thin, spaced black capitals, a breeze-block screen, string lights and potted plants; inside, a white room with
pink ceiling panels, hanging vines, wooden and red metal chairs, busy with friends and families eating together.
The photos show a loaded ramen bowl in a creamy white broth, a cappuccino in a red cup and an iced milk coffee.
Customers say the food is tasty and very affordable, the owner is friendly, the place is always busy, and it is
a great spot for lunch and hanging out with friends. Google rating 4,6 from 227 reviews. No phone or website
in the listing.

## Visitors and what they need
| Visitor | Arrives wanting to | The page must let them |
|---|---|---|
| Friends planning lunch together (reviews 1) | know if the food is good and affordable, and what there is | see the ramen and the menu groups with example prices within one scroll |
| A family planning a meal out (photos) | know there is room for a group and that it's open | see today's hours in the first screen; see the room in "Suasana NUWA" |
| A local or first-timer coming from Maps (listing) | find the place and check it's open now | tap "Petunjuk Arah" and read "Buka hari ini 11.00–22.00" in the first screen |
| [MOCKUP] Students and young workers nearby | a cheap lunch or a coffee after class or work | see prices and the lunch set quickly |
| [MOCKUP] Takeaway iced-coffee customer | check the drinks and get in touch | see "Ngopi di NUWA" and the WhatsApp button (once a number exists) |

## Primary actions
1. Petunjuk Arah (Google Maps link from the listing)  2. WhatsApp (placeholder until the owner gives a number)  3. Read the menu ("Menu andalan" starts directly under the hero; no extra button)
The first screen shows action 1 without scrolling, on a phone.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: an Instagram link (none known yet) and a live
open/closed status (hours are the same every day, so the static "Buka hari ini" line is enough).

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is "Makan siang enak, ngopi santai, harga bersahabat di Pasar Rebo" a good line for the top of the page?
2. Is NUWA an everyday place where people come for lunch and stay for a second coffee?
3. Is the food mainly casual Japanese (ramen) alongside cafe drinks?
4. Are students and young workers from around Gongseng and Pasar Rebo a big part of your customers?
5. Do many customers pick up an iced coffee to take away?
6. Are your ramen called "Ramen Kuah Creamy" and "Ramen Pedas", at about Rp 35rb–45rb? (If not, what are the real names and prices?)
7. Do you serve rice bowls (chicken katsu, chicken teriyaki) at about Rp 30rb–38rb?
8. Do you serve nasi goreng and mie goreng at about Rp 25rb–32rb?
9. Do you serve snacks to share (kentang goreng, cireng, dimsum) at about Rp 18rb–28rb?
10. Are the coffee prices right: Es Kopi Susu Gula Aren Rp 22rb, Cappuccino or Café Latte Rp 25rb–28rb, Americano Rp 20rb?
11. Do you serve matcha latte, chocolate, lychee tea and lemon tea at about Rp 20rb–28rb?
12. Do you have a lunch set (ramen or rice bowl plus es teh) at about Rp 40rb?
13. Is the ramen drawing right (creamy broth, grilled meat, mince, two egg halves, corn, nori, narutomaki, spring onion, chilli oil)? Is the grilled meat chicken?
14. Is "kamu" the right way to talk to customers, or do you prefer "Anda"?
15. Are the section texts fine, including the closing line "Mampir ke NUWA hari ini, ya."?
16. Can you give us a WhatsApp number for the WhatsApp button?
17. Can you send photos of the facade, the dining room, the ramen and the coffee to replace the drawings and placeholders?
18. Can you send your real menu with prices?
19. Do you have an Instagram account we should link?
