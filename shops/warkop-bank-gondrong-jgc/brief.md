## Personality
A proper Jakarta warung kopi: a red cart hung with rows of sachet coffee and drinks, an
umbrella, tables and plastic chairs in the shade of mango trees, where people sit for hours and
the long-haired crew keeps the jokes coming (reviews 2, 3, 4; photos). There's an indoor room
too, with patterned wallpaper, red tables and a speaker (photos), and customers have sat
lesehan-style on colourful mats (photos).
- [MOCKUP] The JGC neighbourhood's front porch, from the first coffee at dawn to the last one at midnight.
