## Tone of voice
Plain-spoken, reassuring and respectful. Says what the office can help with and how to reach it,
in everyday words, with legal terms explained in brackets.
- Site language: Bahasa Indonesia, for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"` (project rule for Indonesian shops)
- Polite Indonesian addressing the reader as "Anda", warmer than a corporate firm.
- Keep within the Indonesian advocates' code of ethics: no "terbaik", "pasti menang", "100% berhasil" or similar, no case results, no client names. A lawyer may not guarantee a client will win (Kode Etik Advokat Indonesia, Pasal 4c), and advertising purely to attract attention is restricted (Pasal 8).
