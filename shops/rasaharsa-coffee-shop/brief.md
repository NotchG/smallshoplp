## Personality
A homey neighbourhood coffee-and-food spot set in what feels like a converted house, where the baristas like to talk you through the beans and regulars come back with family (reviews 1, 2, 4, 5). Charcoal slatted walls, a fern wall and warm wood keep it relaxed rather than trendy (photos).
- The baristas explain the character of the coffee to guests (reviews 1)
- Small, house-like space turned into a cafe, with outdoor seating and a room for private functions (reviews 3, 4)
- The counter shelf holds a siphon, moka pots, a French press and Turkish copper pots, so the bar plays with brewing methods (photos)
- A glass case at the counter has displayed photo prints with a QR e-catalogue for an exhibition, and a T-shirt sign (photos)
- [MOCKUP] A place that treats coffee seriously but never makes you feel you ordered wrong
- [MOCKUP] Open from breakfast to late, so it works for a morning kopi, a laptop afternoon and a dinner with family
