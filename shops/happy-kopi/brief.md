# Happy Kopi

## Basics
- Type: Coffee shop; also listed as cafe (type)
- Address: Ruko Cleon Park, Jl. Jakarta Garden City Boulevard No. 37, Cakung Timur, Cakung, Jakarta Timur 13910 (listing)
- Phone: 0813-9972-8000 (listing)
- Hours: Mon–Sat 08:00–21:00 · Closed Sunday (hours)
- Price level: Moderate (listing)
- Google rating: 4.9 from 28 reviews (listing)
- Shares a shopfront and sign with Kantin Sari Rasa ("KANTIN SARI RASA | HAPPY KOPI") (photos). One reviewer says to head downstairs for bakso campur, so Happy Kopi's seating seems to be upstairs, with the kantin below (review 3, photos).

## Personality
A bright, cheerful little coffee shop where the cups say "keep smile and be happy" (photos), and
reviewers keep coming back to the same three things: comfortable, friendly, tasty (reviews 1, 2, 4, 5).
The room is cream and sage green, with long green banquettes, arched doorways and rounded wall
cutouts (photos).
- [MOCKUP] An everyday spot rather than a destination: the kind of place you settle into for a morning of work and a kopi susu, then order lunch without leaving.
- [MOCKUP] Sweet and a little playful, matching the cup slogan and menu names like "Bana-Nagih".

- [MOCKUP] Office workers and residents along JGC Boulevard on weekdays, since the shop opens at 08:00 and closes Sundays.

## Tone of voice
Cheerful, warm and simple, like the cup: "keep smile and be happy" (photos). Short, friendly
sentences, nothing fancy.
- Site language: Bahasa Indonesia, for all copy, buttons, hours, the draft banner and `lang="id"` (project rule for Indonesian shops)
- Menu names stay exactly as on the board, including English ones (Happy Chicken Rice, Happy Platter, Bana-Nagih, Potato Croquette Mozarella) (photos). The cup slogan stays in English as the shop writes it.
- [MOCKUP] Casual Jakarta-cafe Indonesian that addresses the reader as "kamu".

## Visual direction
- Palette mood: warm cream walls, sage and olive green, pale wood, a soft yellow glow from the counter lights, with terracotta-brown and mustard circles as accents; cream ceramics with a brown rim and a pale green plate (photos)
- Type mood: friendly and rounded; simple uppercase like the "HAPPY KOPI" cup mark, with a light, casual line for the slogan (photos)
- Feel: a sunny, rounded room that makes an ordinary weekday a bit nicer.
- Shapes: arches and half-circles appear all over the room (doorway, wall cutouts, wall graphics) and can carry into the layout (photos).
