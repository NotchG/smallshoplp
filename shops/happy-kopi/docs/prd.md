# PRD: Happy Kopi mockup site

## Goal
A one-page mockup that makes Happy Kopi's owner say "that's us" and want it.
Success for the mockup: the owner recognises their shop within five seconds (their cup stamp,
their sage-and-cream room, their arches), and the checklist questions are few enough to answer
in one conversation.

Language: everything on the page is Bahasa Indonesia (`lang="id"`), including buttons, hours,
the draft banner and alt text. Exceptions, kept exactly as the shop writes them: menu names
from the board (Happy Chicken Rice, Happy Platter, Bana-Nagih, Potato Croquette Mozarella, ...)
and the cup slogan "keep smile and be happy".

## The shop in one paragraph
Happy Kopi is a bright, cheerful coffee shop in Ruko Cleon Park No. 37 on Jl. Jakarta Garden
City Boulevard, Cakung, Jakarta Timur, sharing a shopfront and sign with Kantin Sari Rasa. It is
open Monday to Saturday 08:00–21:00 and closed Sunday, moderately priced, and rated 4.9 from 28
Google reviews. The room is cream and sage green with long green banquettes, pale wood tables,
arched doorways and rounded wall cutouts; iced milk coffees come in clear cups stamped
"HAPPY KOPI · keep smile and be happy". People come to work from the cafe and to eat, and keep
praising the same things: comfortable and cool, super friendly service, really good coffee (the
butterscotch especially) and tasty food. Besides coffee, butterscotch, hot latte and matcha
latte, the menu board lists rice plates, platters, snacks and gorengan with prices.

## Out of scope
Online ordering, booking backends, CMS, accounts, analytics, real photos (placeholders only
unless the owner provided them), multiple pages. Also: Kantin Sari Rasa's menu (including
bakso campur), stating that Happy Kopi is upstairs, any rating breakdown or review dates.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Is the menu board (dishes and prices) still current?
2. Is Happy Kopi upstairs, and should the page say so?
3. Should the page mention Kantin Sari Rasa downstairs?
4. Is "Toast" the right name for the toast at Rp 25rb, or should we use its full name from the menu?
5. Is iced kopi susu gula aren on your menu, and is it your everyday house drink?
6. Do kopi susu drinks start at Rp 20rb?
7. Do flavoured lattes (butterscotch, matcha) start at Rp 25rb?
8. Can we show a price for the hot latte? (If yes, what is it?)
9. Do you have Wi-Fi and power outlets for guests?
10. Does 0813-9972-8000 take WhatsApp orders?
11. Are you on GrabFood, and can you send us your GrabFood link?
12. Is it right to describe Happy Kopi as a place to settle in for a morning of work and stay for lunch?
13. Is a casual tone that calls the reader "kamu" right for you?
14. Are the short intro lines in each section OK as written?
15. Are the drawings of the drinks and dishes OK as placeholders until you send photos of them?
16. Is it OK to show short paraphrases of your Google reviews (no names)?
